Q: CDISC CT 在哪儿？

A (summary delivered in chat):

CDISC Controlled Terminology is developed by CDISC but PUBLISHED/HOSTED by NCI-EVS (National
Cancer Institute – Enterprise Vocabulary Services). Three ways to get it:

- CDISC landing page (overview + links): https://www.cdisc.org/standards/terminology/controlled-terminology
- The actual downloadable files (the source of truth), per standard, on the NCI-EVS FTP site —
  folders for SDTM, ADaM, CDASH, SEND, Define-XML, etc., in txt/xls/pdf/odm formats, versioned
  quarterly: https://evs.nci.nih.gov/ftp1/CDISC/
- Search individual terms / C-codes in the NCI Term Browser (NCIt) — the CDISC Terminology
  subset: https://nciterms.nci.nih.gov (CDISC published subsets).
- Programmatic access: CDISC Library API (https://www.cdisc.org/cdisc-library) — used by tools
  like Pinnacle 21.

So: maintained jointly CDISC + NCI-EVS, files live on the NCI-EVS FTP, versioned ~quarterly.
