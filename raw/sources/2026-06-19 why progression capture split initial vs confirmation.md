Human pasted a progression-tracking table's column headers and asked 为什么这么分:
- Date Progression Detected (Visit)
- Criteria for Initial Progression
- Timing for Confirmation of Progression
- Criteria for Documentation of Disease Progression on Confirmatory Scan

Answer given (content marker):

The table operationalizes the CONFIRMED-progression paradigm (PCWG3 bone "2+2 rule"; same shape as iRECIST iUPD→iCPD). The whole point: separate "first sign of progression" from "confirmed true progression," because a single scan can show APPARENT progression that isn't real — bone-scan flare (early apparent worsening that is actually response/healing), immunotherapy pseudoprogression, and ordinary measurement noise. If you called PD on the first scan you'd wrongly pull patients who are actually benefiting. So progression is a TWO-STEP event, and the four columns are exactly those two steps × (when / what):

1. Date Progression Detected (Visit) = WHEN / at which visit the first apparent progression appeared. Captured separately because the rPFS progression DATE is back-dated to THIS first scan (PCWG3), not the confirmation scan — so the initial date is the one that scores the event.
2. Criteria for Initial Progression = WHAT triggered the initial call (e.g. ≥2 new bone lesions; or RECIST PD in soft tissue). Progression must be criteria-based and mode-specific, not gestalt.
3. Timing for Confirmation of Progression = WHEN the confirmatory scan is due (an interval later / next scheduled scan, e.g. ≥6 weeks). The interval is what lets transient flare/pseudoprogression resolve — confirmation too soon defeats the purpose.
4. Criteria for Documentation on Confirmatory Scan = WHAT counts as confirmed on that second scan (e.g. ≥2 ADDITIONAL new lesions = the second "2" of the 2+2 rule; iCPD). Only a confirmed event becomes the rPFS progression.

So "why split this way": initial vs confirmation (two visits) and date vs criteria (when vs what), because (a) you must filter flare/pseudoprogression/noise via a confirmatory scan after a delay, and (b) the recorded progression date (back-dated to initial detection) differs from the confirmation date, so both must be captured distinctly. It is the CRF form of the 2+2 / iUPD→iCPD logic; feeds rPFS.
