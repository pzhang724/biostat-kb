# PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)

**PSA (prostate-specific antigen, 前列腺特异性抗原)** is a glycoprotein (糖蛋白) released (secreted, 分泌) by prostate epithelial cells (上皮细胞). It is secreted into semen (精液) at high concentration; only a small amount leaks into the blood. Clinically, "PSA" means the **serum PSA** (血清 PSA) level (ng/mL) measured from a blood sample. A rising serum value means more PSA is leaking out as the structure of the prostate tissue is disrupted (破坏) — by cancer, inflammation, or BPH (benign prostatic hyperplasia, 良性前列腺增生). Total, free, and complexed (结合型) forms are reported; the free/total ratio helps separate cancer from BPH in the grey zone (灰区). (Different from **PSMA**, a target on the cell surface used for imaging — see [[PSMA PET-CT]].) It is the most widely used **biomarker** in prostate cancer — for screening (筛查) and monitoring, and in clinical trials as an efficacy and progression endpoint. A rising PSA at castrate testosterone (去势水平睾酮) is part of how [[Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC)|mCRPC]] is defined.

As a trial endpoint:

- **PSA response** — a fall of ≥50% from baseline.
- **PSA progression** — defined by the **PCWG3** (Prostate Cancer Working Group 3) criteria.
- Usually reported together with imaging-based endpoints (RECIST, bone scan / PET).

## What high vs low PSA means

PSA is **not specific to cancer** — it reflects activity/disruption of the prostate epithelium in general, so a level is a warning flag, not a diagnosis.

- **High PSA** — can signal prostate cancer, but also BPH, prostatitis (前列腺炎) / inflammation, or recent ejaculation (射精) / instrumentation (器械操作: biopsy 活检, catheter 导尿管, DRE digital rectal exam 直肠指检). It leads to further tests, not a final answer.
- **Low PSA** — usually reassuring (令人放心); also pushed down by androgen-deprivation therapy (ADT) and 5-α-reductase inhibitors (5α 还原酶抑制剂; finasteride 非那雄胺 roughly halves PSA, so the reading must be doubled).
- **Rough anchor** (粗略参考值) — <4 ng/mL traditionally "normal", 4–10 a grey zone, >10 higher suspicion. But thresholds depend on age and context, and are used less and less, in favor of kinetics (动态变化).

In trials the **kinetics** matter more than any single value: a falling PSA / a low lowest point after treatment (nadir, 最低值) shows response (PSA response = ≥50% fall); a rising PSA shows progression or recurrence (复发) (per [[PCWG3 Criteria]]); and PSA doubling time (倍增时间) / time to nadir are prognostic (预后指标).

**ORR based on PSA** — an objective response rate (客观缓解率) defined by a PSA rule instead of RECIST imaging: the proportion of patients with a confirmed PSA fall of ≥50% from baseline (**PSA50**; PSA30/PSA90 are also reported). Useful in prostate cancer because many patients (especially mCRPC) have mainly bone disease or [[Measurable vs Non-Measurable Disease (RECIST)|non-measurable disease]], where RECIST-based ORR cannot be evaluated. It is still a binary proportion endpoint (responders / patients with an evaluable baseline PSA), analyzed like any ORR (point estimate + exact CI, confidence interval 置信区间; derived per patient via [[Best Overall Response]]). PCWG3 caveat (注意): PSA response is a surrogate, not a replacement for radiographic response or OS — report it (e.g. a waterfall plot 瀑布图 of the maximum PSA fall), but don't use it alone as the primary efficacy measure. Caveat: PSA is a **[[Surrogate Endpoint|surrogate]]** — its changes don't always follow OS, and other anti-cancer therapy easily confounds (混杂) them.

PSA is exactly the kind of efficacy measure that concomitant or subsequent treatment easily contaminates (污染): read its changes in light of what other anti-cancer therapy the patient received (see [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]]).
