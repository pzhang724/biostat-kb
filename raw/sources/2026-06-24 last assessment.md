Answer captured:

- Last Assessment: the final usable pain assessment recorded for a subject — in practice the last
  one BEFORE an intercurrent event ends clean efficacy data (typically before rescue medication,
  or before early withdrawal), or the last scheduled assessment in the AUC window for a completer.
  It is the anchor value that LOCF carries forward, and it marks the cutoff of a subject's usable
  data within the pain endpoint window. Post-rescue / post-withdrawal scores are usually replaced
  by imputation rather than counted, so the "last assessment" is what defines where real
  observation stops and imputation begins.
