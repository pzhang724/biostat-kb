# Progression-Free Survival (PFS) and Overall Survival (OS)

The two main **time-to-event** (事件发生时间 / survival) efficacy endpoints in oncology — different from the **response-based** endpoints (ORR objective response rate, DCR disease control rate, DoR duration of response) that come from [[Best Overall Response]].

## PFS — Progression-Free Survival (无进展生存)

Time from **randomization** to the **first PD (progressive disease) or death from any cause, whichever comes first**; patients with neither event are **censored** (删失). It captures the **delay of progression**.

- Reads out **earlier** than OS — you don't have to wait for deaths.
- **Caveats:** depends on the **assessment schedule** ([[Schedule of Assessments|SoA]]) → interval censoring (区间删失) + evaluation-time bias (评估时间偏倚); needs **blinded independent central review (BICR)** to limit ascertainment bias (确认偏倚); censoring rules depend on the **estimand** (a subsequent therapy is an [[Intercurrent Event]]). Progression is defined by [[RECIST 1.1]] / [[PCWG3 Criteria]]; the radiographic version **rPFS** is used in prostate cancer.

## OS — Overall Survival (总生存)

Time from **randomization** to **death from any cause**. The **gold-standard true endpoint** — objective, clear (unambiguous), and **not** dependent on assessments.

- **Caveats:** needs **long follow-up**, many death events, and a large sample; it is **confounded / diluted** (稀释) by subsequent therapies and crossover (交叉).

## How they relate

OS is the true **clinical benefit**. **ORR / DoR / DCR / PFS** are **earlier readouts** that act as [[Surrogate Endpoint|surrogate endpoints]] for OS (PFS is validated in some tumours, not all). This is the [[Anti-Tumour Activity|activity]] → clinical-benefit ladder. An efficacy concept used across [[Oncology]].
