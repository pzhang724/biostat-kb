Q: 什么是 T2-high asthma?

A:
- T2-high asthma = an asthma endotype（内型，by mechanism）driven by **type 2 inflammation** — adaptive
  Th2 cells + innate ILC2 cells producing the type 2 cytokines IL-4, IL-5, IL-13.
- "High" = those pathways are active, shown by elevated T2 biomarkers: blood/airway eosinophils, FeNO,
  IgE, TARC.
- Umbrella over allergic (atopic) and eosinophilic asthma; tends to respond well to inhaled
  corticosteroids (ICS) and to type 2 biologics (anti-IL-5, anti-IgE, anti-IL-4Rα/IL-13).
- Contrast T2-low / non-T2 asthma — neutrophilic or paucigranulocytic, low T2 biomarkers, poorer ICS
  response, few targeted options.
- Endotype vs phenotype: phenotype = observable cluster (allergic, eosinophilic, late-onset); endotype =
  the underlying mechanism. T2-high names the mechanism.
- Why it matters for trials: it defines the enrichment population — biomarker thresholds (eosinophils
  ≥150/≥300 cells/µL, high FeNO) select likely responders; the whole asthma-biologic field rests on
  phenotyping patients as T2-high.
