Q: 转移灶是说的原始地方对吧?(is the metastatic lesion the original place?)

A:
- No — it's the opposite. The metastatic lesion (转移灶) is the NEW site the cancer spread TO, not where it started.
- 原发灶 / 原发肿瘤 (primary tumor) = the original site where the cancer began. For prostate cancer that's the prostate.
- 转移 (zhuǎnyí) literally = transfer / move; so 转移灶 = the "moved-to" focus, the destination. Metastasis = cancer cells break off the primary, travel (blood/lymph), and seed secondary tumors elsewhere. In prostate cancer the common 转移灶 are bone and lymph nodes.
- So: 原发灶 = origin; 转移灶 = secondary/destination sites. A patient's "multiple lesions" = the primary + its metastases.
- Tie-in: PSA is systemic, so it reflects activity from BOTH the primary and all metastatic sites at once.
