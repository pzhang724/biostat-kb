# Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)

The **Numeric Rating Scale** (数字评分量表) — a patient-reported 0-10 pain scale (0 = no pain, 10 = worst pain imaginable) — assessed in two conditions in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- **NRS-R** — at rest (静息): rated while the patient is still.
- **NRS-M** — with movement (活动/负重) / on activity: rated during a standardized movement such as weight-bearing or ambulation (走动). Usually higher and more clinically meaningful for functional recovery; sometimes called NRS-A.

Serial NRS scores are the raw input integrated into the [[Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)]] primary endpoint. Often collected as an [[ePRO (Electronic Patient-Reported Outcome)]]. Part of the [[Acute Pain]] thread.
