A (summary delivered in chat):

This is an anti-emetic (止吐) premedication / supportive-care regimen — drugs given to prevent
nausea and vomiting (恶心呕吐), typically around a radioligand-therapy infusion.

- Dexamethasone (地塞米松): a corticosteroid (糖皮质激素) used as an anti-emetic (and anti-
  inflammatory) premedication.
- Domperidone (多潘立酮) / Metoclopramide (甲氧氯普胺): prokinetic dopamine D2-receptor
  antagonists — classic anti-emetics; the "/" means either one (often by site availability;
  domperidone less CNS penetration, metoclopramide more available).
- "or institutional anti-emetic regimen": the site may instead use its own hospital standard
  anti-emetic protocol — gives flexibility while ensuring prophylaxis happens.

Why here: in 177Lu-PSMA radioligand therapy the therapy is co-infused with amino acids
(arginine/lysine) for renal protection, which very commonly cause nausea/vomiting; so an
anti-emetic premedication is standard. Prophylaxis (预防性给药), not treatment after the fact.
