# Opioid-Free Rate

Proportion of subjects requiring **no opioid** (阿片类药物) rescue analgesia (补救镇痛) during the assessment window — a key secondary endpoint demonstrating the **opioid-sparing** (阿片节约) effect of an analgesic in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- Partners with **total opioid consumption** (often in morphine milligram equivalents, 吗啡毫克当量) and **time to first opioid rescue**.
- Rescue-opioid use is a classic [[Intercurrent Event]] — it confounds the underlying pain reading, so the estimand must specify how it's handled.
- Rescue medication is captured as [[Prior, Concomitant, and Subsequent Treatment]]; opioid rescue is the permitted [[Prohibited and Rescue Medication|rescue medication]] in pain trials.
