Q: 做 trial 的时候 evaluate safety 指的是什么；evaluate tolerability 指的是什么？

A:
- **Safety (安全性)** = what harm the drug does — the objective medical risk. Measured by adverse
  events (AEs), serious AEs, lab abnormalities, vital signs, ECG/QTc, deaths; characterized by the
  nature, frequency, severity (CTCAE grade), and causality of those events. Question it answers:
  "Does the drug injure the body, and how badly?"
- **Tolerability (耐受性)** = how well the patient can put up with the drug and stay on treatment as
  planned — whether the side effects are manageable enough to keep dosing. Measured by dose
  reductions, dose interruptions/delays, and discontinuations due to AEs, plus treatment
  compliance/adherence. Question it answers: "Can the patient stand to keep taking it on schedule?"
- Relationship: safety = the objective harm profile; tolerability = the practical/subjective ability
  to live with it and stay on the regimen. A drug can be safe (no serious organ damage) but poorly
  tolerated (constant nausea/fatigue → people stop), or fairly well tolerated yet carry rare serious
  safety risks. They are usually reported together ("safety and tolerability") as the typical primary
  objective of phase I.
