# Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC)

**转移性去势抵抗性前列腺癌.** Read the term in three pieces:

- Prostate cancer grows on **androgens** (雄激素; mainly **testosterone**, 睾酮), which signal through the **androgen receptor (AR, 雄激素受体)**. First-line (一线) systemic (全身) treatment is **androgen deprivation therapy (ADT, 雄激素剥夺治疗)** — medical or surgical "castration" (去势) that lowers testosterone to a **castrate level (去势水平, <50 ng/dL)**.
- **Castration-sensitive (去势敏感, mHSPC/mCSPC)** — the disease still responds to ADT. (mHSPC = metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer; mCSPC = metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer.)
- **Castration-resistant (CRPC, 去势抵抗)** — the disease gets worse (rising [[PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)|PSA]] and/or progression on imaging, 影像学进展) **even though** testosterone is at castrate level; the cancer has found ways to switch AR signalling back on.
- **Metastatic (转移性)** — spread beyond the prostate, most often to **bone** and lymph nodes (淋巴结).

So **mCRPC = metastatic + getting worse at castrate testosterone** — an advanced state with a poor prognosis (预后差). It is the population for **ARPIs** (androgen receptor pathway inhibitors, 雄激素受体通路抑制剂: abiraterone 阿比特龙, enzalutamide 恩扎卢胺), taxane chemotherapy (紫杉类化疗: docetaxel, cabazitaxel), **[[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA-617**, radium-223 (镭-223), and PARP inhibitors (PARP 抑制剂). The [[PCWG3 Criteria]] were designed for this setting. Endpoints are usually rPFS (radiographic progression-free survival, 影像学无进展生存期), OS (overall survival, 总生存期) and PSA response.

The central disease state of prostate cancer in [[Oncology]].
