# Lutetium-177 (177Lu)

**¹⁷⁷Lu (镥-177)** is one of the most-used **therapeutic radionuclides** (治疗用放射性核素) in radioligand (放射性配体) therapy. Its key feature: it gives off **two kinds of radiation at the same time**.

- **β⁻ particles (β 粒子, therapeutic, 治疗用)** — travel only a short distance in tissue (~0.5–2 mm), so they put their energy near the place where the drug binds → they kill the tumour and mostly spare tissue farther away. Well suited to small or widely spread (disseminated, 播散的) lesions (病灶).
- **Low-energy γ photons (γ 光子, imageable, 成像用)** — SPECT (single-photon emission computed tomography, 单光子发射计算机断层成像) can detect them, so you can **image and do [[Dosimetry]] with the same therapeutic drug** (good for "theranostics", 诊疗一体化).
- **Half-life (半衰期) ≈ 6.6–6.7 days** — long enough for the drug to spread through the body and stay there.
- **Removed through the kidneys (renally excreted, 经肾排泄)** → the kidney limits the dose (dose-limiting, 剂量限制性); bone marrow (骨髓) is also at risk (see creatinine 肌酐 / MDS, myelodysplastic syndrome 骨髓增生异常综合征, on [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]]).
- Example drugs: **¹⁷⁷Lu-PSMA-617** (prostate cancer), **¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE** (neuroendocrine tumours, 神经内分泌肿瘤, via [[Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT)]]).

**Contrast:** ²²⁵Ac (actinium-225, 锕-225) is an **α-emitter** (α 粒子发射体; stronger, with an even shorter range). ⁶⁸Ga (gallium-68, 镓-68) is the **PET imaging partner** (diagnostic, 诊断用 — same PSMA target, on [[PSMA PET-CT]]).

A key biomedical entity (实体) in [[Oncology]] radioligand programs.
