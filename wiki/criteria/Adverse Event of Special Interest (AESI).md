# Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)

**AESI (特别关注的不良事件)** — a **pre-specified** adverse event (or group) the sponsor flags in the protocol for **proactive, closer surveillance**, because of a known/suspected link to the drug or its class, a regulatory concern, or a class-effect signal worth characterizing. Key point: an AESI gets special handling **even if it is not "serious"** by the SAE definition.

## How it differs from ordinary AE / SAE

| | Trigger | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **AE** | any untoward event | collected + graded ([[CTCAE Grade]]) |
| **SAE** | **seriousness** (death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability…) | expedited reporting |
| **AESI** | **interest** — pre-defined in protocol | targeted extra data (questionnaire / labs / follow-up), often expedited reporting **even if non-serious**, dedicated safety summaries |

AESI is about **characterizing a specific risk**, not just measuring severity. It can overlap with [[Dose-Limiting Toxicity (DLT)]] in dose-finding.

## Radiation AESIs (PSMA radioligand trial)

Tied to the radiation mechanism — they cluster at the organs where the radioligand deposits dose. The protocol's list:

- **Prolonged myelosuppression (持续骨髓抑制)** — red-marrow irradiation; "prolonged" = the persistent, slow-to-recover cytopenia is the concern, not transient dips.
- **Nephrotoxicity (肾毒性)** — kidney is dose-limiting (renal excretion).
- **Leukemia and myelodysplastic syndromes (白血病 / 骨髓增生异常综合征, MDS/AML)** — long-latency radiation-induced marrow malignancy; flagship long-term AESI for radioligand therapy.
- **Other secondary malignancies (其他继发性恶性肿瘤)** — any other radiation-induced second primary cancer.
- **Prolonged xerostomia (持续口干)** — salivary glands express PSMA → off-target irradiation. See [[Xerostomia]].
- **Hyperthyroidism / hypothyroidism (甲亢 / 甲减)** — thyroid dysfunction; this is **why TSH is on the chemistry panel** ([[Laboratory Safety Panels (Hematology, Chemistry, Coagulation, Urinalysis)]]).

Note the **"prolonged"** qualifier and the secondary-malignancy / thyroid entries: the special interest is the **persistent and long-latency** harms of the radiation mechanism — exactly what needs committed up-front surveillance, extra data, and long-term follow-up to characterize.
