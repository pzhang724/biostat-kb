# Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)

The area under the curve (AUC, 曲线下面积) of pain intensity (疼痛强度) over time, from 0 to 72 hours after surgery/dose — the primary endpoint for total (cumulative) pain burden in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- Built from repeated [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]] scores, integrated by the **trapezoidal rule** (梯形法则) over the fixed window.
- **Lower AUC = less total pain.** It captures the whole pain course (trajectory, 轨迹), not a single timepoint.
- **0–72h** is chosen because acute pain after surgery peaks in the first 2–3 days.
- Closely related to **SPID** (Sum of Pain Intensity Differences, 疼痛强度差值总和) — the time-weighted sum of changes from baseline.

Used as the primary efficacy measure in the [[Acute Pain]] thread; see also [[Schedule of Assessments]] for the timing of the pain assessments it integrates.
