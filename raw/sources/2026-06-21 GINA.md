Q: GINA 是啥?

A:
- GINA = Global Initiative for Asthma（全球哮喘防治创议）. A guideline body (launched 1993, WHO / US
  NHLBI origin) that publishes the annually-updated GINA Strategy Report — the global reference for
  asthma diagnosis, assessment, and management.
- It defines: diagnosis; assessment of asthma control (symptom control banded well-controlled /
  partly controlled / uncontrolled, plus future-risk) and severity (mild/moderate/severe, defined
  retrospectively by the treatment step needed to keep control); and stepwise treatment Steps 1–5,
  ICS-based, with the ICS-formoterol reliever paradigm.
- It is a clinical practice guideline, not a regulator — not legally binding (vs FDA/EMA), but widely
  adopted worldwide.
- Trial relevance: GINA steps define background therapy + severity for eligibility (e.g. "severe asthma
  on GINA Step 4–5"); its control definitions inform endpoints; biologics are studied as add-on at the
  high steps.
