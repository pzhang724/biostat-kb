# Asthma Patient-Reported Outcomes (Symptom Diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ, EQ-5D)

**Patient-reported outcomes（患者报告结局, PRO）** capture the patient's own experience — symptoms, control, quality of life — alongside objective lung function and biomarkers. Usually **secondary / key-secondary endpoints**.

- **Asthma symptom diary（哮喘症状日记）** — a daily eDiary of daytime / nighttime symptoms, rescue-medication（缓解药）use, and night awakenings (sometimes peak flow). Aggregated into symptom scores and "well-controlled days"; feeds the control questionnaires.
- **Asthma Control Questionnaire-6 (ACQ-6, 哮喘控制问卷)** — a validated 6-item questionnaire scoring **asthma control** (symptoms + rescue use; the 6-item version drops the FEV1 item). Score **0–6, lower = better control**; minimal clinically important difference（最小临床重要差异, MCID）≈ 0.5.
- **Asthma Quality of Life Questionnaire (AQLQ, 哮喘生活质量问卷)** — a **disease-specific** QoL instrument across symptoms / activity-limitation / emotional / environmental domains. Score **1–7, higher = better**; MCID ≈ 0.5.
- **EQ-5D (European Quality of Life-5 Dimensions, 欧洲五维健康量表)** — a **generic** (not asthma-specific) health-utility instrument: 5 dimensions → a single **utility index** plus a VAS. Used for health-economic / **QALY** analyses and cross-disease comparability (HTA / payer).

So the disease-specific tools (diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ) quantify asthma burden and control; EQ-5D is the generic utility measure for health economics.

**Direction & what each indicates** (watch the flipped scales):
- **Symptom diary** — **lower score / fewer symptoms = better**; indicates day-to-day symptom burden and rescue use.
- **ACQ-6** — **lower = better** (0–6); indicates **asthma control**.
- **AQLQ** — **higher = better** (1–7); indicates disease-specific **quality of life**.
- **EQ-5D** — **higher utility = better**; indicates **general health state / utility** for QALY analyses.

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area; complements the efficacy endpoints [[Annualized Asthma Exacerbation Rate (AAER)]] and [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]].
