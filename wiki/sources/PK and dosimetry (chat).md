# PK and dosimetry (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 PK and dosimetry.md`. Follow-up after PSMA theranostics.

- Question: what are PK and dosimetry in clinical trials?
- PK (pharmacokinetics): what the body does to the drug — ADME; concentration-time curve; params Cmax/Tmax/AUC/t½/CL/Vd/Css; used for dose selection, dose-proportionality, DDI, exposure-response. Phase I is PK-driven.
- Dosimetry: for radiopharmaceuticals — absorbed radiation dose (Gy) to tumors and organs at risk (kidneys, salivary glands, marrow) via serial SPECT/PET → time-activity curve → cumulated activity → absorbed dose (MIRD). Used for safety dose-limits and tumor dose-response; personalized activity selection.
- Shared idea: both quantify "how much reaches where over time" — PK in drug conc, dosimetry in absorbed dose. Ties to 177Lu-PSMA theranostics.
