Q: Order the tumour response categories: CR, PR, SD, NED, NE, PD.

A: Ordered best -> worst by tumour burden / favorability:

CR ≈ NED > PR > SD > PD   — and NE sits OUTSIDE the ordering.

- CR (Complete Response) — all known disease gone (best).
- NED (No Evidence of Disease) — no detectable disease; ranks alongside CR. But it's a clinical / post-surgical term (e.g. patient rendered disease-free after resection, or entered with no measurable disease), NOT a standard RECIST timepoint category — RECIST uses CR. It surfaces in CDISC response controlled terminology / adjuvant settings.
- PR (Partial Response) — substantial shrinkage (≥30% in SoD).
- SD (Stable Disease) — neither PR nor PD.
- PD (Progressive Disease) — growth / new lesions (worst).
- NE (Not Evaluable) — can't be assessed (missing/inadequate assessment). Not better or worse — it's "unknown", so it is not on the better/worse scale; in BOR it's handled separately (NE only if no evaluable post-baseline assessment).

So the favorability rank is CR/NED > PR > SD > PD, with NE off-axis.
