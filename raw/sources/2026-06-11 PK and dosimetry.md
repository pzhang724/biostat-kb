Q: 讲一下临床试验里的 PK 和 dosimetry 都是个啥?

A:

PK (Pharmacokinetics, 药代动力学)
- "What the body does to the drug" — the time course of drug in the body: Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion (ADME). (Contrast PD, pharmacodynamics = what the drug does to the body.)
- Measured by sampling blood (sometimes urine) at set times after dosing -> a concentration-time curve.
- Key parameters: Cmax (peak conc), Tmax (time to peak), AUC (area under curve = total exposure), t1/2 (half-life), CL (clearance), Vd (volume of distribution), steady-state Css for repeat dosing.
- Why in trials: characterize exposure, check dose-proportionality, pick the dose/schedule, assess accumulation, drug-drug interactions (DDI), special populations (renal/hepatic impairment), and feed EXPOSURE-RESPONSE (PK/PD) analyses. Phase I is largely PK-driven.

Dosimetry (剂量测定) — specific to RADIOPHARMACEUTICALS / radioligand therapy & radiation
- Quantifies the ABSORBED RADIATION DOSE (in Gray, Gy) delivered to tumors and to normal/critical organs by a radioactive drug (e.g. 177Lu-PSMA-617) — the radiation analogue of PK exposure.
- How: serial quantitative imaging (SPECT/PET, e.g. of 177Lu or a 68Ga companion) +/- blood sampling at several timepoints -> a time-activity curve per organ/tumor -> integrate to cumulated activity -> convert to absorbed dose (Gy) using dose factors (MIRD methodology).
- Organs at risk: kidneys (often dose-limiting for PSMA/peptide therapies), salivary glands, bone marrow, liver.
- Why in trials: stay under organ dose-limit thresholds (safety), confirm tumor gets a meaningful dose, and relate tumor absorbed dose to response (dose-response). Supports personalized/activity selection in radioligand therapy.
- Link: ties to PSMA theranostics — the same PSMA target imaged on PSMA PET/CT is what 177Lu-PSMA binds, and dosimetry quantifies the radiation that delivers.

Shared idea: both quantify "how much reaches where, over time" — PK in drug concentration, dosimetry in absorbed radiation dose.
