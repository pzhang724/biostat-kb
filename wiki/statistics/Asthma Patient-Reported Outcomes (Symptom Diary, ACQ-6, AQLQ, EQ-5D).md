# Asthma Patient-Reported Outcomes (Symptom Diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ, EQ-5D)

**Patient-reported outcomes (患者报告结局, PRO)** capture the patient's own experience — symptoms, control, quality of life — next to the objective lung function and biomarkers. Usually **secondary / key-secondary endpoints**.

- **Asthma symptom diary (哮喘症状日记)** — a daily eDiary (electronic diary) of daytime / night-time symptoms, use of rescue medication (缓解药), and waking up at night (sometimes peak flow, 峰流速). Added up into symptom scores and "well-controlled days"; it feeds the control questionnaires.
- **Asthma Control Questionnaire-6 (ACQ-6, 哮喘控制问卷)** — a validated 6-item questionnaire that scores **asthma control** (symptoms + rescue use; the 6-item version leaves out the FEV1 item). Score **0–6, lower = better control**; the minimal clinically important difference (最小临床重要差异, MCID) ≈ 0.5.
- **Asthma Quality of Life Questionnaire (AQLQ, 哮喘生活质量问卷)** — a **disease-specific** QoL (quality of life) instrument (量表) across the symptoms / activity-limitation / emotional / environmental domains. Score **1–7, higher = better**; MCID ≈ 0.5.
- **EQ-5D (European Quality of Life-5 Dimensions, 欧洲五维健康量表)** — a **generic** (not asthma-specific) health-utility (健康效用) instrument: 5 dimensions → a single **utility index** plus a VAS (visual analogue scale, 视觉模拟评分). Used for health-economic / **QALY** (quality-adjusted life year, 质量调整生命年) analyses and for comparing across diseases (HTA, health technology assessment 卫生技术评估 / payers 支付方).

So the disease-specific tools (diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ) measure asthma burden and control; EQ-5D is the generic utility measure for health economics.

**Direction and what each shows** (watch out for the flipped scales):

- **Symptom diary** — **lower score / fewer symptoms = better**; shows day-to-day symptom burden and rescue use.
- **ACQ-6** — **lower = better** (0–6); shows **asthma control**.
- **AQLQ** — **higher = better** (1–7); shows disease-specific **quality of life**.
- **EQ-5D** — **higher utility = better**; shows **general health state / utility** for QALY analyses.

These are collected electronically as [[ePRO (Electronic Patient-Reported Outcome)]] (a daily diary plus questionnaires at visits).

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area; goes with the efficacy endpoints [[Annualized Asthma Exacerbation Rate (AAER)]] and [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]].
