From chat, 2026-06-19 (follow-up to IRB/IEC + financial disclosure)

Q: If a committee (IRB/IEC) member is involved in another trial of the same type, which category does that fall in?

A: It's a conflict of interest — specifically a NON-FINANCIAL / competing-professional-interest conflict, distinct from the financial COI (equity/payments/proprietary interest under 21 CFR 54).

Why it's a conflict even without money:
- Competing interest — their own same-type trial competes for the same patients / scientific space; they could be biased for or against a rival program.
- Confidentiality — reviewing a competitor's protocol/IB exposes them to confidential proprietary information of a program they compete with.
- Intellectual / career bias — a stake in a particular scientific approach.

Handling: the member must DISCLOSE it and RECUSE from that study's review and vote (leave the room for that item). Same recusal mechanism as a financial conflict.

If they ALSO hold equity / outcome-linked payment / a proprietary interest in the competing product, it additionally becomes a FINANCIAL conflict — but the "I run a similar/competing trial" part by itself is the non-financial/competing-interest category.

So two COI buckets: FINANCIAL (money/equity/proprietary — also the 21 CFR 54 disclosure axis) and NON-FINANCIAL / competing-professional-interest (a same-type or rival trial, relationships, intellectual stake). A competing trial lands in the second.
