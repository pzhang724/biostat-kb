# Study drug vs anti-cancer therapy (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 study drug vs anti-cancer therapy.md`.

- Question: difference between the experimental drug and "anti-cancer therapy."
- Two levels: (1) pharmacology — study drug *is* a kind of anti-cancer therapy (subset, not parallel); (2) trial data — "anti-cancer therapy" is a data category for treatments *other than* the study drug.
- Study drug / IP: the single product being evaluated; SDTM **EX** (EC if needed).
- Prior anti-cancer therapy: drives eligibility, washout, line of therapy, refractory status; SDTM **CM** (drugs) / **PR** (radiation, surgery).
- Concomitant anti-cancer therapy: usually prohibited; contaminates efficacy/safety attribution.
- Subsequent anti-cancer therapy: key OS confounder after discontinuation.
- Core framing: study drug = the studied "independent variable"; other anti-cancer therapy = confounders to control/explain.
