# PET (Positron Emission Tomography)

**PET (正电子发射断层成像)** — **functional / molecular** imaging: it shows a biological **process**, not only anatomy (解剖结构).

## How it works (原理)

1. Inject a **radiotracer (放射性示踪剂)** = a biologically active molecule labelled with an isotope (同位素) that gives off **positrons** (¹⁸F-FDG = a glucose look-alike, 葡萄糖类似物; ⁶⁸Ga / ¹⁸F PSMA ligands, 配体).
2. The isotope decays (衰变) and gives off a **positron (β⁺, 正电子** = the antiparticle 反粒子 of the electron).
3. The positron travels a tiny distance and hits an electron → **annihilation (湮灭)** → their mass turns into **two 511 keV gamma photons** that fly out in exactly **opposite directions (180°)**.
4. A ring of detectors (探测器) catches the two photons at the same moment = **coincidence detection (符合探测)** → the event lies on the line between those two detectors (line of response, 响应线).
5. Millions of these lines rebuild (reconstruct, 重建) a 3-D map of where the tracer collected = where the tracked process is happening. Uptake (摄取) is measured as **SUV (standardized uptake value, 标准摄取值)**.

**What the signal means depends on the tracer:**

- **¹⁸F-FDG** (fluorodeoxyglucose, 氟代脱氧葡萄糖) — tumours use more glucose (Warburg effect, 瓦博格效应) → they "light up". General oncology PET.
- **PSMA ligand** — binds PSMA on prostate-cancer cells → see [[PSMA PET-CT]].

**Why it is combined with CT (PET/CT):** PET shows function, but the anatomy is blurry (模糊); CT gives the anatomical map → function + anatomy in one image. Compare with [[MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)]] / CT, which show anatomy only.

Theranostic (诊疗一体化) link: the same PSMA target is used to image *and* to treat ([[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)]]-PSMA; radiation measured by [[Dosimetry]]). An imaging modality (成像方式) used in [[Radiological Assessment]] within [[Oncology]].
