# Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT)

**PRRT (肽受体放射性核素治疗)** — a type of **radioligand therapy** (放射性配体治疗) in which a **radiolabeled peptide (放射性标记肽)** binds a **receptor on the cell surface (细胞表面受体)** that tumour cells have too much of (overexpressed, 过表达). It delivers targeted internal radiation from inside the cell.

- **Mechanism** — a tumour-seeking **peptide (ligand, 配体)** carries a therapeutic radionuclide (治疗用放射性核素). The peptide docks onto (结合) a receptor that the cancer cell overexpresses, so the **β⁻ (or α)** radiation is deposited at or inside that cell. A subtype of [[Radiopharmaceutical Therapy (RPT) and External Beam Radiation Therapy (EBRT)|RPT]] — internal, **systemic (全身)** radiation, targeted by biology.
- **Classic example (archetype, 典型代表)** — **somatostatin analogues (生长抑素类似物)** (DOTATATE / DOTATOC) labeled with [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]] (or ⁹⁰Y, yttrium-90 钇-90), targeting **somatostatin receptors (SSTR, 生长抑素受体)**, which are overexpressed on **gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumours (GEP-NET, 胃肠胰神经内分泌肿瘤)**.
- **Approved drug** — **¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE (Lutathera)**, for SSTR-positive GEP-NETs; pivotal (关键性) trial **NETTER-1**.
- **Theranostic pairing (诊疗一体化)** — **⁶⁸Ga-DOTATATE PET** images and selects patients (same SSTR target); **¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE** treats — diagnosis + therapy on one target. Same logic as the imaging/therapy split on [[PSMA PET-CT]].
- **Relation to PSMA therapy** — the same theranostic logic as **¹⁷⁷Lu-PSMA-617** in prostate cancer. "PRRT" classically means the **peptide→receptor (SSTR / NET)** class, while PSMA-617 is a small-molecule ligand (小分子配体). Same concerns about the **kidney / marrow limiting the dose (剂量限制)** as other ¹⁷⁷Lu drugs.

## Indication — the target population, qualifier by qualifier

The classic PRRT label population (说明书适应人群) reads *"inoperable, progressive, SSTR+ neuroendocrine tumours of gastroenteric or pancreatic origin"* (the ¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE / Lutathera, NETTER-1 population). Each word is an **eligibility gate (入排条件)**:

- **neuroendocrine tumours (NET, 神经内分泌肿瘤)** — tumours from **neuroendocrine cells (神经内分泌细胞)** (cells that release hormones, spread throughout the body); usually slow-growing.
- **of gastroenteric or pancreatic origin (GEP-NET, 胃肠胰来源)** — located in the GI (gastrointestinal, 胃肠道) tract or the pancreas (胰腺) — the classic PRRT indication.
- **somatostatin receptor-positive (SSTR+, SSTR 阳性)** — the tumour **overexpresses SSTR**, confirmed by **⁶⁸Ga-DOTATATE PET** (or the older octreotide 奥曲肽 scan). This is the target the peptide binds — **no receptor, no PRRT**. It is the selection / theranostic gate.
- **inoperable (不可切除)** — cannot be cured or removed by surgery. Surgery is the curative (根治性) option for localized NET, so "inoperable" means you have moved on to **systemic therapy (全身治疗)**.
- **progressive (进展性)** — the disease is documented (有记录证实) to be growing/worsening (often after an earlier somatostatin analogue). Progression justifies stepping up to PRRT instead of watchful waiting (观察等待).

All together: a NET in the gut or pancreas that cannot be cut out, is actively growing, and shows the SSTR target — exactly the patient PRRT is designed for. (See [[Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria]] — each qualifier is an inclusion gate.)
