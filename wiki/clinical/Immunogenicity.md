# Immunogenicity

**Immunogenicity (免疫原性)** — a drug's ability to trigger (provoke, 引发) an **immune response** in the patient: the body sees the drug as foreign (外来物) and reacts, mainly by making **anti-drug antibodies (ADA, 抗药抗体)**. Mainly a concern for **biologics (生物制品)** — large molecules: monoclonal antibodies (单克隆抗体), fusion proteins (融合蛋白), antibody-drug conjugates (ADC, 抗体偶联药物), enzymes (酶) — and even more for non-human or chimeric (嵌合) ones. **Small-molecule drugs rarely cause it.**

Some ADA are **neutralizing antibodies (NAb, 中和抗体)** that block the drug's active site / function.

## Why it matters

- **Efficacy** — ADA/NAb neutralize the drug or make the body clear it (清除) faster → lower exposure, lost activity.
- **Safety** — **hypersensitivity (超敏反应)** / infusion reactions, up to anaphylaxis (过敏性休克) (see [[Infusion-Related and Hypersensitivity Reactions]]); rarely, ADA cross-react (交叉反应) with a protein the body makes itself (**endogenous protein**, 内源性蛋白) — this is serious.
- **PK** (pharmacokinetics, 药代动力学) — ADA change clearance / half-life, so exposure can drift (漂移) over time (see [[Pharmacokinetics (PK)]]).

## How it's assessed

A **tiered (分层) bioanalytical (生物分析) strategy**: screening assay (筛选检测) → confirmatory assay (确证检测) → **titer (滴度)** → neutralizing-antibody assay. Reported as ADA **incidence/prevalence** (发生率/流行率), titer, **timing** (treatment-emergent 治疗期间新出现 / treatment-boosted 治疗后增强), and **persistence** (持续性). It is a specialty lab feed from an external vendor (外部供应商) — **[[CRF vs Non-CRF (External) Data|non-CRF]]** data (CRF = case report form, 病例报告表). In CDISC it maps to the **SDTM IS domain** (Immunogenicity Specimen Assessments — see [[SDTM, SDTM IG, and Conformance Rules]]).

**Regulatory** — FDA/EMA/ICH have guidance on immunogenicity assessment. It is required in biologic development and central to **biosimilars (生物类似药)** (immunogenicity compared against the reference product, 参照药).

Part of [[Oncology]]. Relevant to antibody / ADC drugs (see [[Targeted Therapy and Tissue-Agnostic Biomarkers]], [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]]).
