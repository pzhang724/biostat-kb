# Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC)

转移性去势抵抗性前列腺癌. The term reads in three pieces:

- Prostate cancer grows under **androgens (testosterone, 睾酮)** signalling through the **androgen receptor (AR, 雄激素受体)**. First-line systemic treatment is **androgen deprivation therapy (ADT, 雄激素剥夺治疗)** — medical/surgical "castration" lowering testosterone to a **castrate level (去势水平, <50 ng/dL)**.
- **Castration-sensitive (去势敏感, mHSPC/mCSPC)** — disease still responds to ADT.
- **Castration-resistant (CRPC, 去势抵抗)** — disease progresses (rising [[PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)|PSA]] and/or radiographic progression) **despite** castrate testosterone; the cancer has found ways to reactivate AR signalling.
- **Metastatic (转移性)** — spread beyond the prostate, most often to **bone** and lymph nodes.

So **mCRPC = metastatic + progressing at castrate testosterone** — an advanced, poor-prognosis state. It is the population for **ARPIs** (abiraterone, enzalutamide), taxane chemo (docetaxel, cabazitaxel), **[[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA-617**, radium-223, and PARP inhibitors; the [[PCWG3 Criteria]] were designed for this setting; endpoints are typically rPFS, OS, and PSA response.

The central disease state of prostate cancer in [[Oncology]].
