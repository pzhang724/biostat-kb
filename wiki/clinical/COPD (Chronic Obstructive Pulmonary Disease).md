# COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)

**COPD (慢性阻塞性肺疾病, 慢阻肺)** — a disease that keeps getting worse over time (progressive, 进行性), with airflow limitation (气流受限) that is mostly **not reversible** (不可逆). It is mainly caused by smoking or pollution (污染) exposure, and is usually diagnosed in older adults. It includes chronic bronchitis (慢性支气管炎) and emphysema (肺气肿). The inflammation (炎症) is driven more by neutrophils (neutrophilic, 中性粒细胞性) than by eosinophils (eosinophilic, 嗜酸性粒细胞性).

Contrast with [[Asthma]]:

- **Reversibility of the obstruction (阻塞)** — asthma is reversible; COPD is not fully reversible.
- **Typical cause and age of onset (发病)** — asthma: allergy (过敏), childhood; COPD: smoking, older adults.

## Treatment

Treats the airflow limitation first. The backbone (基础治疗) is a combination of long-acting bronchodilators (长效支气管扩张剂): LABA (long-acting beta-agonist, 长效β2受体激动剂) + LAMA (long-acting muscarinic antagonist, 长效抗胆碱能药). [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]] are added only in certain subgroups (亚组) — people with frequent exacerbations (急性加重) or high blood eosinophils — not first-line (一线) as in asthma. Non-drug measures are central: smoking cessation (戒烟), pulmonary rehabilitation (肺康复), vaccination (接种疫苗), and oxygen therapy (氧疗) for severe disease with low blood oxygen (hypoxemic, 低氧血症).
