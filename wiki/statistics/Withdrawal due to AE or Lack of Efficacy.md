# Withdrawal due to AE or Lack of Efficacy

Two key reasons why a subject stops treatment early (discontinues). Both are [[Intercurrent Event]]s, but they are handled differently:

- **Lack of efficacy** (疗效不足) — the drug is not controlling the pain. Conservative imputation (保守填补) — WOCF (worst observation carried forward) / BOCF (baseline observation carried forward) — assumes the pain continues or is at its worst, i.e. the patient is counted as a failure.
- **Adverse event** (不良事件, AE) — withdrawal caused by toxicity, not by pain. The pain at withdrawal may have been fine, so it is handled differently from a dropout for lack of efficacy.

These map onto the [[Estimand]] ICE (intercurrent event) strategies — e.g. **composite** (count the ICE as a failure), **treatment-policy**, **hypothetical**. The chosen handling is then put into practice by an [[Imputation and Carried-Forward Methods (LOCF, WOCF, wWOCF)]] rule. Part of the [[Acute Pain]] thread.
