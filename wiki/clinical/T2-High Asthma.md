# T2-High Asthma

**T2-high asthma** (2 型炎症高表型哮喘) = an asthma **endotype (内型 — defined by mechanism 机制, not just by appearance)** driven by **type 2 inflammation**: adaptive (适应性) **Th2 (2 型辅助 T 细胞)** cells plus innate (固有) **ILC2 (2 型固有淋巴细胞)** cells. They produce the type 2 cytokines (细胞因子) **IL-4, IL-5, IL-13** (IL = interleukin, 白细胞介素).

- **"High"** = those pathways (通路) are active, shown by high [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]] — eosinophils, IgE, TARC — and [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]].
- It is the **umbrella (总称) over allergic (atopic, 特应性) and eosinophilic (嗜酸性) asthma**, and usually **responds well to** [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]] and to **type 2 biologics (生物制剂)** (anti-IL-5, anti-IgE, anti-IL-4Rα/IL-13).
- **Contrast — T2-low / non-T2 asthma**: neutrophilic (中性粒细胞性) or paucigranulocytic (少粒细胞性), low T2 biomarkers, weaker response to ICS, few targeted options.

**Endotype vs phenotype (内型 vs 表型)**: a *phenotype* is a group of features you can observe (allergic, eosinophilic, late-onset 晚发); an *endotype* is the mechanism underneath. **T2-high names the mechanism.**

**Why it matters in trials**: it defines the **enrichment (富集) population** — biomarker thresholds (阈值; e.g. eosinophils ≥150 / ≥300 cells/µL, high FeNO) pick the patients likely to respond. The whole asthma-biologic field depends on classifying (phenotyping) patients as T2-high.

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area; read with [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]], [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]], and [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]].
