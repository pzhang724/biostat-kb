From chat, 2026-06-19 (follow-up to Best Overall Response / why ORR is on BOR)

Q: So you mean, as long as a CR/PR ever happened, [the patient stays a responder]?

A: Yes — as long as a CONFIRMED CR/PR is achieved BEFORE first progression, that patient's BOR is CR/PR and they count as a responder permanently; later progression does NOT remove them from the ORR numerator.

This is the asymmetry that "best-so-far" creates:
- A good response, once earned (and confirmed), is LOCKED IN — BOR only moves up, never down.
- A later PD only stops the window (you can't improve further after progression); it does not downgrade an already-achieved response.

The one gate: it must be CONFIRMED. A single unconfirmed PR/CR (no repeat assessment >=4 weeks later, in ORR-primary/non-randomized trials) does not count. So "ever happened" really means "ever achieved AND confirmed, before first PD".
