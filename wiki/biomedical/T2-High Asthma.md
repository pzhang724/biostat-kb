# T2-High Asthma

**T2-high asthma**（2 型炎症高表型）= an asthma **endotype（内型 — defined by mechanism, not just appearance）** driven by **type 2 inflammation**: adaptive **Th2（2 型辅助 T 细胞）** plus innate **ILC2（2 型固有淋巴细胞）** cells producing the type 2 cytokines（细胞因子）**IL-4, IL-5, IL-13**.

- **"High"** = those pathways are active, evidenced by elevated [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]] — eosinophils, IgE, TARC — and [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]].
- It is the **umbrella over allergic (atopic, 特应性) and eosinophilic (嗜酸性) asthma**, and tends to **respond well to** [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]] and to **type 2 biologics（生物制剂）** (anti-IL-5, anti-IgE, anti-IL-4Rα/IL-13).
- **Contrast — T2-low / non-T2 asthma**: neutrophilic（中性粒细胞性）or paucigranulocytic（少粒细胞性）, low T2 biomarkers, poorer ICS response, few targeted options.

**Endotype vs phenotype（内型 vs 表型）**: a *phenotype* is an observable cluster (allergic, eosinophilic, late-onset); an *endotype* is the underlying mechanism. **T2-high names the mechanism.**

**Why it matters in trials**: it defines the **enrichment（富集）population** — biomarker thresholds (e.g. eosinophils ≥150 / ≥300 cells/µL, high FeNO) select likely responders. The whole asthma-biologic field rests on phenotyping patients as T2-high.
