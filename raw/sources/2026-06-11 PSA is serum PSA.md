Q: PSA 指的是血里的 PSA 吗?(follow-up to PSA Q&A)

A:
- Yes. Clinically "PSA" almost always means SERUM PSA — concentration measured from a blood sample, reported in ng/mL.
- Biology: PSA is produced by prostate epithelium and secreted into SEMEN at very high concentration; only a small amount leaks into the bloodstream. The blood level is what is routinely assayed (semen PSA is not used clinically except in forensics).
- A rising serum PSA is interpreted as more PSA leaking into blood due to disrupted prostate tissue architecture (cancer, inflammation, BPH).
- Forms reported: total PSA, free PSA, complexed PSA; the free/total ratio helps distinguish cancer from BPH in the grey zone.
