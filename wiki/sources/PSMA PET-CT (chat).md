# PSMA PET-CT (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 PSMA PET CT.md`. Follow-up in the prostate-cancer thread.

- Question: what is PSMA PET/CT?
- PSMA = Prostate-Specific Membrane Antigen, a surface protein on prostate-cancer cells (vs PSA = secreted, blood-measured).
- PSMA PET/CT: inject a PSMA-binding radiotracer, PET shows where cancer cells are, CT gives anatomy. Tracers: 68Ga-PSMA-11, 18F-DCFPyL, 18F-PSMA-1007.
- Uses: staging, detecting biochemical recurrence at low PSA, much more sensitive than CT + bone scan; theranostic pairing with 177Lu-PSMA therapy.
- Caveat: RECIST/PCWG3 built on conventional imaging; PSMA PET sensitivity causes stage migration / discordant early progression; formal PSMA-PET response criteria (e.g. RECIP) still emerging.
