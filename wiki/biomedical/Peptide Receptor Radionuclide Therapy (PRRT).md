# Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy (PRRT)

**PRRT (肽受体放射性核素治疗)** — a form of **radioligand therapy** where a **radiolabeled peptide (放射性标记肽)** binds a **cell-surface receptor (细胞表面受体)** overexpressed on tumour cells, delivering targeted internal radiation from inside the cell.

- **Mechanism** — a tumour-targeting **peptide (ligand, 配体)** carries a therapeutic radionuclide; the peptide docks onto a receptor overexpressed on the cancer cell, so the **β⁻ (or α)** radiation is deposited at/inside that cell. A subtype of [[Radiopharmaceutical Therapy (RPT) and External Beam Radiation Therapy (EBRT)|RPT]] — internal, **systemic (全身)**, biology-targeted radiation.
- **Archetype** — **somatostatin analogues (生长抑素类似物)** (DOTATATE / DOTATOC) labeled with [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]] (or ⁹⁰Y), targeting **somatostatin receptors (SSTR, 生长抑素受体)** overexpressed on **gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumours (GEP-NET, 胃肠胰神经内分泌肿瘤)**.
- **Approved drug** — **¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE (Lutathera)**, for SSTR-positive GEP-NETs; pivotal trial **NETTER-1**.
- **Theranostic pairing (诊疗一体化)** — **⁶⁸Ga-DOTATATE PET** images/selects patients (same SSTR target); **¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE** treats — diagnostic + therapeutic on one target. Same logic as the imaging/therapy split on [[PSMA PET-CT]].
- **Relation to PSMA therapy** — same theranostic logic as **¹⁷⁷Lu-PSMA-617** in prostate cancer; PRRT classically denotes the **peptide→receptor (SSTR / NET)** class, while PSMA-617 is a small-molecule ligand. Same **kidney / marrow dose-limiting (剂量限制)** concerns as other ¹⁷⁷Lu agents.

Part of [[Oncology]]. See [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)]], [[Dosimetry]], [[PSMA PET-CT]].
