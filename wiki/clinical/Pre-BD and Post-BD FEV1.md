# Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1

**FEV1** = forced expiratory volume in 1 second (一秒用力呼气容积) — the core lung-function measure from **spirometry** (肺量计).

- **Pre-BD** = pre-bronchodilator (使用支气管扩张剂前) — measured *before* an inhaled bronchodilator.
- **Post-BD** = post-bronchodilator — measured *after* a short-acting β2-agonist (短效β2受体激动剂, e.g. salbutamol / albuterol 沙丁胺醇).
- The difference is **reversibility (可逆性)**: a post-BD FEV1 increase of **≥12% and ≥200 mL** shows reversible airflow limitation. It supports an asthma diagnosis and separates asthma from the more fixed airflow limitation of COPD.

In asthma trials, pre-BD FEV1 is often an **inclusion criterion** (入选标准) and a **baseline / efficacy endpoint**; shown (demonstrated) reversibility is often required for entry.

**Direction**: a higher FEV1 = better airflow / lung function, so raising baseline pre-BD FEV1 is the treatment goal. But a *larger* pre→post reversibility is **not** "better" — it is a diagnostic signal of reversible airway disease, not a treatment target. With good control, baseline FEV1 goes up and there is less for a rescue bronchodilator to reverse.

**Change from baseline of pre-BD FEV1** is one of the most common primary / key efficacy endpoints in asthma trials: the pre-rescue FEV1 at a landmark visit (关键访视, e.g. week 24) minus the baseline value (in mL or L). Pre-BD is used (not post-BD) because it shows the patient's own **ongoing underlying lung function**, not yet lifted by a bronchodilator — so a rise shows the drug improved baseline airway function in a **lasting** (sustainable, 持续的) way. The trial reads the **difference between treatment and control** in change from baseline, usually by **MMRM** (mixed model for repeated measures, 重复测量混合模型), adjusting for baseline FEV1.

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area. A companion biomarker is [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]]; the partner spirometry measure is [[Forced Vital Capacity (FVC)]] (via the FEV1/FVC ratio); exacerbations (急性加重) are tracked by the [[Annualized Asthma Exacerbation Rate (AAER)]].
