# GINA (Global Initiative for Asthma)

**GINA** = Global Initiative for Asthma (全球哮喘防治创议) — a guideline (指南) body. It started in 1993 from a collaboration of the WHO (World Health Organization) and the US NHLBI (National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute). It publishes the **GINA Strategy Report, updated every year** — the global reference for asthma diagnosis, assessment and management.

What it defines:

- **Diagnosis** — confirming asthma (symptoms that vary + airflow limitation that varies / reversibility 可逆性, see [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]]).
- **Assessment of control (控制评估)** — symptom control is put in three bands (分级): **well-controlled / partly controlled / uncontrolled** (the questions behind tools like [[Asthma Patient-Reported Outcomes (Symptom Diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ, EQ-5D)|ACQ]]), plus **future risk** (exacerbations 急性加重, decline).
- **Severity (严重度)** — mild / moderate / severe, defined **looking back** (retrospectively, 回顾性) by the **treatment step** needed to keep the patient controlled.
- **Stepwise treatment (阶梯治疗) — Steps 1–5**, built on [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]], with the ICS-formoterol (福莫特罗) reliever (缓解药) approach; type 2 biologics are added at the high steps.

It is a **clinical practice guideline, not a regulator** (监管机构) — not legally binding (法律约束力) like FDA / EMA rules, but used worldwide.

**Trial relevance**: GINA **steps define background therapy + severity for eligibility** (e.g. "severe asthma on GINA Step 4–5"); its control definitions shape endpoints; biologics are studied as **add-on** (加用) at the high steps, where the population is mostly [[T2-High Asthma]].

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area.
