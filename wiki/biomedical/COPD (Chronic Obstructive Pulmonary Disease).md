# COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)

Progressive disease with largely irreversible airflow limitation (气流受限), mainly caused by smoking/pollutant exposure, usually diagnosed in older adults. Encompasses chronic bronchitis and emphysema (肺气肿), driven more by neutrophilic (中性粒细胞性) than eosinophilic (嗜酸性粒细胞性) inflammation.

Contrast with [[Asthma]]: reversibility of obstruction (asthma reversible vs. COPD not fully reversible) and typical cause/age of onset (allergy/childhood vs. smoking/older adult).

## Treatment

Treats airflow limitation first: a long-acting bronchodilator combination (LABA/LAMA) is the backbone. [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]] is only added in specific subgroups (frequent exacerbators, high blood eosinophils) — not first-line like in asthma. Non-drug measures are central: smoking cessation (戒烟), pulmonary rehabilitation (肺康复), vaccination; oxygen therapy for severe hypoxemic disease.
