# Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity (LAST)

**LAST (局麻药全身毒性)** — a serious adverse reaction (不良反应) that happens when a **local anesthetic** (局部麻醉药 — bupivacaine 布比卡因, lidocaine 利多卡因, ropivacaine 罗哌卡因) reaches toxic levels in the blood of the whole body (systemic circulation, 体循环). Causes: injecting into a blood vessel by accident (**intravascular injection**, 血管内注射), or too high a dose / too much absorption. It harms two systems:

- **CNS** (central nervous system, 中枢神经系统): early signs are numbness around the mouth (perioral numbness, 口周麻木), ringing in the ears (tinnitus, 耳鸣), a metallic taste, and agitation (烦躁). It can progress to **seizures** (癫痫发作), then CNS depression (抑制) or coma (昏迷).
- **Cardiovascular** (心血管): low blood pressure (hypotension, 低血压), arrhythmias (心律失常), conduction block (传导阻滞), **cardiac arrest** (心脏骤停). Bupivacaine is especially toxic to the heart (cardiotoxic, 心脏毒性).

Relevant in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials that use local anesthetics for pain control after surgery (post-surgical analgesia, 术后镇痛) — e.g. long-acting bupivacaine injected into the wound (wound infiltration, 伤口浸润) or a nerve block (神经阻滞). Management: stop the injection, control the airway (气道) and the seizures, and give IV (intravenous, 静脉) **lipid emulsion** (脂肪乳剂, "lipid rescue" / Intralipid).

A key safety event to monitor — usually an [[Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)]] and, when severe, a [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)]]. Part of the [[Acute Pain]] thread.
