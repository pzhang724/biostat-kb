# Laboratory Safety Panels (Hematology, Chemistry, Coagulation, Urinalysis)

The full lab list from the trial's [[Schedule of Assessments]]. Four panels: **hematology** (血液学; what the marrow makes), **blood chemistry** (血生化; organ function + electrolytes 电解质 + metabolism), **coagulation** (凝血; bleeding risk + how well the liver makes clotting factors), **urinalysis** (尿常规; a cheap screen for kidney / bladder / infection). In a PSMA radioligand trial these are mostly **safety-monitoring** labs, repeated each cycle — and three groups (marrow, kidney, bladder) are exactly where the radioligand deposits its dose. The logic of entry-threshold *direction* (which ones want high vs low) is on [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]]; this page lists each parameter. Whether each lab is run at a central lab (electronic data feed) or locally (entered in the eCRF) is on [[CRF vs Non-CRF (External) Data]].

## Hematology Panel (marrow output)

| Parameter | 中文 | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Hemoglobin | 血红蛋白 | how much oxygen the blood can carry; low = anemia (贫血) |
| Red blood cell count | 红细胞计数 | number of red cells (RBC); read together with Hgb |
| White blood cell count | 白细胞计数 | total immune cells; low = infection risk, high = infection/inflammation |
| Platelet count | 血小板计数 | clotting cells; low = bleeding risk |
| Reticulocyte percent | 网织红细胞百分比 | % of young (immature) red cells = how fast the marrow is **making new cells** (regenerating, 再生); low retic + anemia = the marrow is not responding (e.g. suppressed by radiation) |

**5-part WBC differential (白细胞分类):**

- **Neutrophil** (中性粒细胞) — bacterial infection / neutropenia (中性粒细胞减少) (the count that has entry floors)
- **Lymphocyte** (淋巴细胞) — viral immunity (抗病毒免疫)
- **Monocyte** (单核细胞)
- **Eosinophil** (嗜酸性粒细胞) — allergy / parasites (寄生虫)
- **Basophil** (嗜碱性粒细胞)

## Blood Chemistry Panel

**Liver** — AST / ALT (转氨酶; hepatocellular injury 肝细胞损伤; ALT is more specific to the liver) · Alkaline phosphatase / ALP (碱性磷酸酶; cholestasis 胆汁淤积 **or** bone — note that bone mets and bone-building activity, osteoblastic 成骨活性, raise it) · LDH (lactate dehydrogenase, 乳酸脱氢酶; non-specific cell turnover 细胞更新 / tumour burden / hemolysis 溶血) · Total & direct bilirubin (总/直接胆红素; direct = conjugated 结合型 → a biliary/excretion problem; indirect → hemolysis / Gilbert syndrome 吉尔伯特综合征) · Total protein (总蛋白) & Albumin (白蛋白; liver synthesis + nutrition / prognosis).

**Kidney** — Creatinine + calculated creatinine clearance (肌酐 + 计算肌酐清除率; CrCl estimates GFR, glomerular filtration rate 肾小球滤过率; the kidney limits the dose of radioligand) · BUN (blood urea nitrogen, 尿素氮; kidney + hydration 水合状态 / protein) · Uric acid (尿酸; also a marker of tumour lysis 肿瘤溶解).

**Electrolytes / acid-base (酸碱平衡)** — Sodium (钠) · Potassium (钾; critical for the heart) · Chloride (氯) · Bicarbonate (碳酸氢盐; acid-base) · Total calcium (总钙; bone mets → hypercalcemia 高钙血症) · Phosphate (磷酸盐) · Magnesium (镁).

**Pancreas (胰腺)** — Amylase (淀粉酶) · Lipase (脂肪酶; more specific to the pancreas); they flag pancreatitis (胰腺炎).

**Muscle / cardiac** — Creatine kinase / CK (肌酸激酶); if it is high, **split it into isoenzymes** (同工酶) to separate skeletal muscle (CK-MM) from heart (CK-MB).

**Endocrine / metabolic** — TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone, 促甲状腺激素; a thyroid screen — it is on the panel because hyper-/hypothyroidism is a radiation [[Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)]]) · Glucose, non-fasted (血糖, 非空腹).

**Thyroid, in more depth:** the thyroid gland makes **T4 / T3** (thyroxine 甲状腺素 / triiodothyronine 三碘甲状腺原氨酸; they set the metabolic rate, 代谢率); the pituitary (垂体) makes **TSH** to tell it to produce them. Because of feedback (反馈), **TSH moves opposite to thyroid hormone** — **high TSH → hypothyroid** (underactive, 功能减退), **low TSH → hyperthyroid** (overactive, 功能亢进). TSH is the **screen**; **free T4/T3** confirm. Immunotherapy, TKIs (tyrosine kinase inhibitors, 酪氨酸激酶抑制剂), and radiation/radioligands can cause thyroid dysfunction → monitored, sometimes an AESI.

## Coagulation

The **clotting cascade** (凝血级联) — how blood forms a clot to stop bleeding. The tests measure clotting **time**, which shows **bleeding risk**.

- **INR** (international normalized ratio, 国际标准化比值) — standardized PT (prothrombin time, 凝血酶原时间); **extrinsic** (外源性) + common pathway; sensitive to **warfarin** (华法林) and to **liver synthesis** (clotting factors are made in the liver, several of them vitamin-K dependent). INR makes PT comparable across labs.
- **PTT / aPTT** ((activated) partial thromboplastin time, 部分凝血活酶时间) — **intrinsic** (内源性) + common pathway; sensitive to **heparin** (肝素) and to missing clotting factors (factor deficiencies, e.g. hemophilia 血友病).

So coagulation does two jobs: **(1) a bleeding-risk** check and **(2) a liver-function** readout (the liver makes the factors). Fibrinogen (纤维蛋白原) / D-dimer (D-二聚体) / platelet count complete it (platelets are in hematology). Used for baseline bleeding risk, monitoring anticoagulants (抗凝药), safety before biopsies/procedures, and signals like DIC (disseminated intravascular coagulation, 弥散性血管内凝血).

## Urinalysis (Dip Stick)

A cheap screen. **Go on to microscopic examination (镜检) only if the dipstick is abnormal** (a "reflex" test: casts 管型, crystals 结晶, cells, bacteria).

- **Blood** (潜血) — hematuria (血尿); kidney/bladder. Relevant because the radioligand leaves through the kidneys → risk of cystitis (膀胱炎).
- **Glucose** (尿糖) — spills into the urine when blood glucose is above the kidney threshold (renal threshold, 肾糖阈) (diabetes).
- **Protein** (尿蛋白) — proteinuria (蛋白尿) = damage to the glomeruli (肾小球) / kidney.
- **Leucocyte test** (白细胞酯酶) — UTI (urinary tract infection, 尿路感染) / inflammation.

Note: urinalysis parameters usually have **no [[CTCAE Grade]] term**, so unlike chemistry/hematology analytes they cannot be CTCAE-graded.

## Radioligand tie-in

Marrow (hematology + reticulocyte), kidney (creatinine/CrCl/BUN + urine protein/blood), and bladder (urine blood → cystitis) are the organs where the dose is deposited — see [[Xerostomia]], [[Dosimetry]], [[Pharmacokinetics (PK)]]. TSH, electrolytes, pancreas and CK are general safety labs. Part of [[Oncology]]; collected per the [[Schedule of Assessments]] grid.
