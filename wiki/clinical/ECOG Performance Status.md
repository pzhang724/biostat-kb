# ECOG Performance Status

A 0–5 scale (ECOG PS = Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status, 体能状态评分) of how much a patient's illness limits daily life. **Higher = worse.**

- **0** — fully active, no limits
- **1** — limited in hard physical activity (strenuous, 剧烈的), but can walk (ambulatory, 能走动) and do light work
- **2** — can walk and is up and about for more than 50% of waking hours, but cannot work
- **3** — limited self-care (自理); in bed or chair for more than 50% of waking hours
- **4** — completely disabled (完全失能), stays in bed (bedbound, 卧床)
- **5** — dead

Used for **eligibility** (入组资格; trials often require ECOG 0–1 or 0–2), **stratification** (分层), and **prognosis** (预后). A clinical assessment used across [[Oncology]], together with [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]].

**"Decline in ECOG"** means the patient's performance status is **getting worse (恶化)**. Watch the direction: higher ECOG = worse, so a *decline in condition* means the **score goes up** (e.g. 1 → 2 → 3). It is a sign of clinical deterioration (临床恶化) or possible disease progression, and is often used as a reason to stop treatment (**discontinuation/withdrawal criterion**, 停药/退出标准) — e.g. a decline to ECOG ≥3 → off treatment. Sometimes it is defined as a drop of ≥1 point from baseline (基线), i.e. the number going up by ≥1. The wording is the confusing part: "decline" (worse) and "number goes up" point the same way.
