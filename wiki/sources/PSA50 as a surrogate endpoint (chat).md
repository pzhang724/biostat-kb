# PSA50 as a surrogate endpoint (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 PSA50 as surrogate.md`. Human checking their mental model; this note records the assessment.

- Human's model: non-measurable tumor → forced to use PSA; PSA drop ⇒ tumor shrank; PSA50 = 1/0; "like a surrogate endpoint."
- Mostly correct. Caveat on the middle step: PSA decline only *correlates* with tumor shrinkage — it doesn't measure size, can fall disproportionately, can show early "PSA flare". Indirect readout, not a measurement.
- PSA50 is indeed binary per patient (confirmed ≥50% decline); PSA-based ORR = proportion of responders.
- "Surrogate endpoint" framing is right — but it's a surrogate *for OS* (the true endpoint); the binary-ness isn't what makes it a surrogate, substituting for the clinical outcome is.
- Key caveat: PSA response is a weak / not-fully-validated surrogate for OS; regulators rely on rPFS + OS, not PSA alone (PCWG3). Validity = treatment effect on marker must capture effect on true endpoint (Prentice spirit).
