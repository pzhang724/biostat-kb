# Forced Vital Capacity (FVC)

**FVC** = forced vital capacity (用力肺活量) — the total amount of air a person can blow out as hard as possible after breathing in fully. Measured by **spirometry (肺量计/肺功能检查)**; reported in litres (or as % of the predicted value, 预计值百分比).

- **Partner to FEV1** (forced expiratory volume in 1 second, 第一秒用力呼气容积). The **FEV1/FVC ratio (一秒率)** is the key marker of obstruction (阻塞). A low ratio (commonly <0.70) = an **obstructive** pattern (asthma / COPD). A normal or high ratio with a low FVC points to a **restrictive (限制性)** pattern.
- In asthma, blocked airflow lowers FEV1 more than FVC, so the **FEV1/FVC ratio falls**.
- In trials: collected together with [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]] as a secondary lung-function measure. The ratio supports diagnosis and severity grading (严重程度分级).

**Direction and meaning**: **higher FVC = better** (more usable lung volume). A low FVC with a *normal* ratio means **restriction**. **FEV1/FVC ratio: lower = worse**, meaning **airflow obstruction** (the asthma pattern). So FVC alone tells you lung size; the ratio tells you obstruction.

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area; read with [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]].
