# Adverse Event Ascertainment

How AEs are actually **found and captured** during a trial. For symptomatic AEs the main channel **is** doctor–patient communication (医患沟通) — but not the only one, and everything passes through the investigator's medical judgment.

## Patient communication — two modes

- **Spontaneous** (自发报告) — the patient volunteers a complaint.
- **Solicited** (问询) — at each visit the investigator actively asks, using an **open-ended, non-leading** question ("any health problems since your last visit?") **rather than reading a symptom checklist** — a checklist would bias / inflate reporting. (Structured checklists/diaries are reserved for specific solicited safety — e.g. vaccine reactogenicity — or for pre-specified [[Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)|AESIs]].)

## Non-communication sources

- **Investigator observation** + [[Physical Examination|physical exam]].
- **Objective tests** — an abnormal [[Laboratory Safety Panels (Hematology, Chemistry, Coagulation, Urinalysis)|lab]] / vital sign / [[12-Lead Resting ECG|ECG]] that the investigator judges **clinically significant** becomes an AE (the data-triggered route).
- **Other** — ePRO / patient diaries, between-visit phone calls, concomitant-medication review (a new con-med can reveal an unreported AE), hospitalization records.

## The investigator adjudicates

Whatever the source, it is the **investigator** who turns it into an AE record — assigning the [[MedDRA]] term, the [[CTCAE Grade|grade]], the [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)|seriousness]], causality, and the [[Action Taken for an Adverse Event|action taken]]. So patient communication is the main *input* for symptoms, but the AE record itself is **investigator-judged and multi-source**.

Ascertainment is continuous across the [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)#Safety reporting period|safety reporting period]]; the recorded *time* is the AE's own onset date, not the visit it surfaced at — see [[SDTM, SDTM IG, and Conformance Rules#Events vs Findings timing — how an AE's occurrence is timed|Events vs Findings timing]]. A cross-cutting safety concept in [[Oncology]].
