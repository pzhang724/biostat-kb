# GINA (Global Initiative for Asthma)

**GINA** = Global Initiative for Asthma（全球哮喘防治创议）— a guideline（指南）body (launched 1993, from a WHO / US NHLBI collaboration) that publishes the **annually-updated GINA Strategy Report**, the global reference for asthma diagnosis, assessment, and management.

What it defines:

- **Diagnosis** — confirming asthma (variable symptoms + variable airflow limitation / reversibility, see [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]]).
- **Assessment of control（控制评估）** — symptom control banded **well-controlled / partly controlled / uncontrolled** (the question set behind tools like [[Asthma Patient-Reported Outcomes (Symptom Diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ, EQ-5D)|ACQ]]), plus **future-risk** (exacerbations, decline).
- **Severity（严重度）** — mild / moderate / severe, defined **retrospectively** by the **treatment step** needed to keep the patient controlled.
- **Stepwise treatment（阶梯治疗）— Steps 1–5**, built on [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]], with the ICS-formoterol reliever paradigm; type 2 biologics are add-on at the high steps.

It is a **clinical practice guideline, not a regulator** — not legally binding (unlike FDA / EMA), but adopted worldwide.

**Trial relevance**: GINA **steps define background therapy + severity for eligibility** (e.g. "severe asthma on GINA Step 4–5"); its control definitions inform endpoints; biologics are studied as **add-on** at the high steps, where the population is largely [[T2-High Asthma]].

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area.
