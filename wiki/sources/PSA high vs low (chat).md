# PSA high vs low (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 PSA high low meaning.md`. Follow-up to [[PSA (chat)]].

- Question: what do high vs low PSA mean?
- PSA is not cancer-specific — reflects prostate epithelial activity generally.
- High: cancer, but also BPH, prostatitis, recent ejaculation, instrumentation → investigate, not diagnose.
- Low: reassuring; also lowered by ADT and 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors (finasteride ~halves PSA).
- Rough anchor: <4 normal, 4–10 grey zone, >10 higher suspicion — age/context-dependent.
- Trials care about kinetics > single value: falling/low nadir = response; rising = progression (PCWG3); doubling time prognostic.
- Surrogate caveat: PSA changes don't always track OS and are confounded by other anti-cancer therapy.

Related follow-up (2026-06-11, `raw/sources/2026-06-11 PSA is serum PSA.md`): "PSA" clinically = **serum** PSA (ng/mL from blood). PSA is secreted into semen at high concentration; only a little leaks into blood, and that blood level is what's measured. Forms: total / free / complexed PSA; free/total ratio helps separate cancer from BPH in the grey zone.
