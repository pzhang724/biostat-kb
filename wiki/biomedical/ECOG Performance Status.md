# ECOG Performance Status

A 0–5 scale (ECOG PS, 体能状态评分) of how much a patient's illness affects daily functioning. **Higher = worse.**

- **0** — fully active, no restriction
- **1** — restricted in strenuous activity but ambulatory, can do light work
- **2** — ambulatory, up >50% of waking hours, cannot work
- **3** — limited self-care, in bed/chair >50% of waking hours
- **4** — completely disabled, bedbound
- **5** — dead

Used for **eligibility** (trials often require ECOG 0–1 or 0–2), **stratification**, and **prognosis**. A cross-cutting clinical assessment in [[Oncology]] alongside [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]].

**"Decline in ECOG"** means the patient's performance status **worsening (恶化)** — mind the direction: higher ECOG = worse, so a *decline in condition* corresponds to the **score going up** (e.g. 1 → 2 → 3). It signals clinical deterioration / possible disease progression, and is often used as a **discontinuation/withdrawal criterion** (e.g. decline to ECOG ≥3 → off treatment); sometimes defined as a drop of ≥1 point from baseline (i.e. the number increasing by ≥1). The wording is the confusing part — "decline" (worse) and "number goes up" point the same way.
