# Chemotherapy

**Chemotherapy (化疗)** — the **cytotoxic (细胞毒性)** systemic-drug type (modality) of cancer treatment: chemical drugs that kill cells by breaking the machinery of **cell division** (细胞分裂).

## Principle (原理)

The defining trait of cancer is fast, uncontrolled growth (proliferation, 增殖), so **cells that divide fast are easier to hurt** (more vulnerable) — chemo uses (exploits) that difference. Main classes, by the step they hit:

- **Alkylating agents (烷化剂)** — cross-link (交联) / damage DNA so it cannot copy itself (replicate).
- **Antimetabolites (抗代谢药)** — fake building blocks that block DNA/RNA synthesis (合成) (e.g. methotrexate 甲氨蝶呤, 5-FU 5-氟尿嘧啶).
- **Anti-microtubule / spindle poisons (微管抑制剂)** — block the mitotic spindle (有丝分裂纺锤体) so the cell cannot split (**taxanes** 紫杉类, e.g. docetaxel; vinca alkaloids 长春花生物碱).
- **Topoisomerase inhibitors (拓扑异构酶抑制剂)** — jam (卡住) the enzymes that unwind DNA.
- **Anti-tumour antibiotics (抗肿瘤抗生素)** — e.g. anthracyclines (蒽环类; DNA intercalation 嵌入 + free radicals 自由基).

Net effect: damage DNA / stop cell division (mitosis, 有丝分裂) → trigger programmed cell death (apoptosis, 细胞凋亡). It is **systemic** (travels everywhere in the blood), unlike local surgery / radiotherapy.

**Why the toxicity:** chemo is **not specific to cancer** — it hits any tissue that renews fast. So the classic side effects are in bone marrow (low blood counts, cytopenias 血细胞减少), the gut lining (GI 胃肠道: mucositis 黏膜炎 / nausea), and hair follicles (hair loss, alopecia 脱发). This lack of selectivity is the core limitation, and the reason targeted and immune therapies were developed.

## Why it is called "therapy" (为什么是 therapy)

- **"-therapy" (疗法 / 治疗)** = a **treatment modality** (治疗方式) given for clinical benefit — the same ending as radio*therapy*, immuno*therapy*, hormone *therapy*. It means "a way of treating disease", not a specific drug.
- **"Chemo-therapy"** literally = "treatment with **chemical** agents". The term comes from **Paul Ehrlich** (early 1900s), for using chemicals to kill the disease agent selectively.
- So it is "therapy" because it is one of the **modalities** of cancer treatment — the systemic-cytotoxic one — together with surgery (手术), radiotherapy (放疗), and newer systemic modalities: targeted therapy, immunotherapy, hormone/endocrine therapy, and **radioligand therapy** ([[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)]]). The name marks it as a treatment *approach* defined by mechanism (chemical/cytotoxic), not by drug name.

One of the systemic anti-cancer-therapy groups — see [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]]. Earlier (prior) taxane chemo defines the treatment line (治疗线) / refractory (难治) status in [[Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC)]]. A treatment modality in [[Oncology]].
