# Meaning of using anti-cancer therapy (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 meaning of using anti-cancer therapy.md`. Follow-up to [[Study drug vs anti-cancer therapy (chat)]].

- Question: what does it mean for a subject to "use anti-cancer therapy"?
- In trial data, it means any cancer-directed treatment *other than* the study drug/IP — a data classification, not a pharmacological one.
- Its meaning depends almost entirely on **timing** relative to the study drug: prior / concomitant / subsequent.
- A bare "anti-cancer therapy used = Yes" flag means little on its own; the load-bearing question is *when* and *at what stage* — that decides whether it is a baseline characteristic, a covariate/confounder, or a signal to explain.
