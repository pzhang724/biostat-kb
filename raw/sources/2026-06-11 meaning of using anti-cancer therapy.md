Q: 使用 anti-cancer therapy 意味着什么呢?(follow-up to study-drug-vs-anti-cancer-therapy Q&A)

A:
- In trial data, "受试者使用了 anti-cancer therapy" = any cancer-directed treatment OTHER THAN the study drug/IP. It's a data classification, not a pharmacological one (pharmacologically the study drug is itself an anti-cancer therapy).
- The meaning depends almost entirely on TIMING relative to the study drug — three mutually exclusive buckets:
  - Prior (入组前): drives eligibility, washout, line of therapy, refractory/resistant status. SDTM CM (drugs) / PR (radiation, surgery).
  - Concomitant (研究期间): usually prohibited; contaminates efficacy/safety attribution; often a protocol deviation.
  - Subsequent (停药后): major OS confounder (crossover, later lines).
- Key point: a bare "anti-cancer therapy used = Yes" flag means little on its own. The load-bearing question is "when, and at what stage relative to the study drug" — that decides whether it's a baseline characteristic, a covariate/confounder, or a safety/efficacy signal needing explanation.
