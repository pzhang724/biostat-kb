# Patient Global Assessment (PGA)

**PGA (患者总体评价)** — a one-question, patient-reported (患者报告) rating of **overall** satisfaction with treatment / pain control: the patient's overall (global) impression of the pain drug (analgesic, 镇痛药) in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- Usually a short categorical scale (e.g. poor / fair / good / excellent, or 0–4).
- Captures the patient's overall impression, not a moment-by-moment pain score; it adds to the more detailed [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]].
- A version rated by the investigator/clinician (Clinician Global Assessment, 医生总体评价) sometimes runs alongside it.
