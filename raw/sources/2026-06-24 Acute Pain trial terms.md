Answer captured:

- AUC(0-72h): Area under the pain-intensity-vs-time curve from 0 to 72 hours post-surgery/dose.
  The cumulative-pain-burden primary endpoint in acute pain trials; computed from serial NRS
  pain scores by the trapezoidal rule over the fixed window. Lower = less total pain. 72h chosen
  because acute post-surgical pain peaks in the first 2-3 days. Closely related to SPID
  (Sum of Pain Intensity Differences).

- NRS-R: Numeric Rating Scale at Rest. 0-10 pain scale (0 = no pain, 10 = worst imaginable),
  rated while the patient is still/at rest.

- NRS-M: Numeric Rating Scale with Movement / on activity. Same 0-10 scale, rated during a
  standardized movement (e.g. weight-bearing, ambulation). Usually higher and more clinically
  meaningful for functional recovery; sometimes called NRS-A (activity).

- OPIOID-FREE: Opioid-free rate. Proportion of subjects requiring NO opioid rescue analgesia
  during the assessment window. Key secondary endpoint showing opioid-sparing effect; partners
  with total opioid consumption (often in morphine milligram equivalents) and time to first
  opioid rescue. Rescue-opioid use is also a classic intercurrent event.

- PGA: Patient Global Assessment. Single-item patient-reported rating of overall treatment
  satisfaction / pain control (e.g. poor / fair / good / excellent, or 0-4). Captures the
  patient's overall impression of the analgesic.

- MPADSS: Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System. Standardized tool for deciding
  when a patient is ready for discharge after ambulatory (day) surgery. Five criteria — vital
  signs, ambulation, nausea/vomiting, pain, surgical bleeding — each 0-2, total 0-10; >=9
  usually = discharge-ready.

- Unilateral: One-sided surgery (one foot only). Required in the bunionectomy model so the pain
  signal isn't confounded by a contralateral procedure.

- osteotomy: Surgical cutting/division of bone to realign it. In the bunionectomy model a first
  metatarsal osteotomy is performed; one of the FDA-standard acute pain surgical models (with
  third molar extraction and abdominoplasty) because it produces predictable, reproducible
  moderate-to-severe post-op pain.
