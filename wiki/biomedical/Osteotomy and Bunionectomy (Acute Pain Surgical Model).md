# Osteotomy and Bunionectomy (Acute Pain Surgical Model)

- **Osteotomy** (截骨术) — surgical cutting/division of a bone to realign it.
- **Bunionectomy** (拇外翻矫正术) — correction of a bunion via a **first metatarsal** (第一跖骨) osteotomy. One of the **FDA-standard acute pain surgical models** (with third molar extraction, 第三磨牙拔除, and abdominoplasty, 腹壁整形术) because it produces predictable, reproducible moderate-to-severe post-op pain.
- **Unilateral** (单侧) — done on one foot only, so the pain signal isn't confounded by a contralateral procedure.

The pain it generates is read out by the [[Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)]] / [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]] endpoints; recovery/discharge by the [[Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System (MPADSS)]]. Anchors the [[Acute Pain]] thread.
