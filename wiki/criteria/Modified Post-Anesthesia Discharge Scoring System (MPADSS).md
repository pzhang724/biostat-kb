# Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System (MPADSS)

A standardized scoring rule for deciding when a patient is ready for **discharge** (出院) after ambulatory (day) surgery (日间手术) — used in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials to assess recovery.

Five criteria, each scored 0-2 (total 0-10):

- **Vital signs** (生命体征)
- **Ambulation** (行走能力)
- **Nausea/vomiting** (恶心/呕吐)
- **Pain** (疼痛)
- **Surgical bleeding** (手术出血)

A total **≥9** usually indicates discharge-ready. Part of the [[Acute Pain]] thread, alongside the [[Osteotomy and Bunionectomy (Acute Pain Surgical Model)]] day-surgery setting.
