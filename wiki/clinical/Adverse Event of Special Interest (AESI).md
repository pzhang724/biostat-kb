# Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)

**AESI (特别关注的不良事件)** — an adverse event (or a group of events), defined in advance (**pre-specified**, 预先规定), that the sponsor marks in the protocol for **active, closer watching** (proactive surveillance, 主动监测). Reasons: a known or suspected link to the drug or its drug class, a regulatory concern, or a class-effect (类效应) signal worth describing. Key point: an AESI gets special handling **even if it is not "serious"** by the SAE (serious adverse event, 严重不良事件) definition.

## How it differs from ordinary AE / SAE

| | Trigger | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **AE** (adverse event, 不良事件) | any unwanted (untoward, 不良的) event | collected + graded ([[CTCAE Grade]]) |
| **SAE** | **seriousness** (death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability…) | expedited (加急) reporting |
| **AESI** | **interest** — defined in advance in the protocol | targeted extra data (questionnaire / labs / follow-up), often expedited reporting **even if non-serious**, dedicated safety summaries |

AESI is about **describing (characterizing) a specific risk**, not only measuring severity. It can overlap with [[Dose-Limiting Toxicity (DLT)]] in dose-finding.

## Radiation AESIs (PSMA radioligand trial)

Tied to the radiation mechanism — they gather at the organs where the radioligand deposits (沉积) its dose. The protocol's list:

- **Prolonged myelosuppression (持续骨髓抑制)** — the red marrow (红骨髓) gets irradiated. "Prolonged" = the concern is low blood counts (cytopenia, 血细胞减少) that last and recover slowly, not short dips.
- **Nephrotoxicity (肾毒性)** — the kidney limits the dose (the drug leaves through the kidneys, renal excretion 经肾排泄).
- **Leukemia and myelodysplastic syndromes (白血病 / 骨髓增生异常综合征, MDS/AML)** — marrow cancer caused by radiation that appears after a long delay (long latency, 长潜伏期); the flagship (最典型的) long-term AESI for radioligand therapy. (AML = acute myeloid leukemia, 急性髓系白血病.)
- **Other secondary malignancies (其他继发性恶性肿瘤)** — any other second primary cancer caused by radiation.
- **Prolonged xerostomia (持续口干)** — the salivary glands carry PSMA → they get irradiated off-target. See [[Xerostomia]].
- **Hyperthyroidism / hypothyroidism (甲亢 / 甲减)** — thyroid dysfunction (甲状腺功能异常); this is **why TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone, 促甲状腺激素) is on the chemistry panel** ([[Laboratory Safety Panels (Hematology, Chemistry, Coagulation, Urinalysis)]]).

Note the word **"prolonged"** and the second-cancer / thyroid entries: the special interest is in the **lasting and late-appearing** (persistent and long-latency) harms of the radiation mechanism — exactly what needs planned up-front monitoring, extra data, and long-term follow-up to describe.
