Q: (病灶的"灶")为什么叫灶?

A:
- 灶 (zào) literally means a cooking stove / hearth / fireplace.
- 病灶 = "disease hearth" — the focal point or seat where disease is concentrated or originates, by analogy to a hearth being the focal source of a fire.
- This mirrors the Western medical term it translates: "focus" (as in "focus of infection/disease"). Latin focus literally means "hearth / fireplace" — the same hearth metaphor independently. English "lesion" comes from Latin laesio = injury, a different root, but the Chinese 病灶 maps to the focus/hearth idea.
- So 灶 captures the sense of a localized central point of disease, not just any injured spot.
