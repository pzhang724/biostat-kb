From chat, 2026-06-19 (follow-up to SAE / SUSAR)

Q: Relationship between SAE and SAR.

A: SAR ⊂ SAE — the only thing SAR adds is **causality**.

- The "event vs reaction" wording is the giveaway: an adverse **event (AE)** is any untoward occurrence, with NO assumption about cause; an adverse **reaction (AR)** is an AE where there is at least a reasonable possibility it was **caused by the drug**. "Reaction" = causality implied.
- Apply "serious" to each: **SAE** = a serious AE (meets a seriousness criterion, cause irrelevant). **SAR** = a serious adverse reaction = an SAE that is also judged **related** to the study drug.
- So every SAR is an SAE; an SAE becomes an SAR once the causality assessment comes back "related". SAEs judged unrelated are SAEs but not SARs.
- Next step up: an SAR that is also **unexpected** (not in the RSI/IB) = SUSAR. Nesting: AE ⊃ SAE ⊃ SAR ⊃ SUSAR.
