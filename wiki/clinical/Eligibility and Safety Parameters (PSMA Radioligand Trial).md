# Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)

The eligibility / safety screen of a PSMA-targeted radioligand prostate-cancer trial. The labs confirm that organs work well enough (organ-function adequacy) and drive monitoring during treatment; the conditions are mostly exclusion criteria (排除标准). Several of them — kidney (creatinine), bladder (cystitis), salivary glands (xerostomia), marrow (MDS) — map to where the radioligand deposits its dose.

## Lab / ECG safety & eligibility parameters (organ function)

Two classes: four "reserve / synthesis" (储备/合成) markers want **high** (entry floor, 下限); five "injury / toxicity" (损伤/毒性) markers want **low** (entry cap, 上限).

| English | 中文 | 高好/低好 | Entry | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) | 中性粒细胞绝对计数 | 高好 high | floor 下限 | low = neutropenia (中性粒细胞减少) → infection (often ≥1.5×10⁹/L) |
| Hemoglobin (Hgb) | 血红蛋白 | 高好 high | floor 下限 | low = anemia (贫血) |
| Platelets | 血小板 | 高好 high | floor 下限 | low = thrombocytopenia (血小板减少) → bleeding |
| Serum albumin | 血清白蛋白 | 高好 high | floor 下限 | low = poor liver synthesis / nutrition / prognosis |
| AST (aspartate aminotransferase) | 天冬氨酸氨基转移酶 (谷草转氨酶) | 低好 low | cap 上限 | high = hepatocellular injury (≤2.5–3× ULN, upper limit of normal) |
| ALT (alanine aminotransferase) | 丙氨酸氨基转移酶 (谷丙转氨酶) | 低好 low | cap 上限 | high = hepatocellular injury |
| Total bilirubin | 总胆红素 | 低好 low | cap 上限 | high = poor excretion / cholestasis (胆汁淤积) (≤1.5× ULN) |
| Creatinine | 肌酐 | 低好 low | cap 上限 | high = poor kidney function (the kidney limits the dose of radioligand) |
| QT interval | QT间期 | 低/短好 low | cap 上限 | long = risk of arrhythmia (torsades) |
| QTc | 校正QT间期 (按心率校正) | 低/短好 low | cap 上限 | same, corrected for heart rate (e.g. QTcF ≤480 ms) |

Grouping by organ: ANC/Hgb/platelets = hematology (marrow reserve); albumin/AST/ALT/bilirubin = liver; creatinine = kidney; QT/QTc = cardiac.

**口诀 (mnemonic):** 身体"产出/储备"的 → 越高越好 (floor)；"损伤/废物"标志 → 越低越好 (cap)。
> **「血、蛋白 要高；肝、肾、心电 要低。」**
> - 要高 (floors): 血 = 三系血细胞 (ANC / Hgb / Plt)、蛋白 = 白蛋白 — 身体造出来、要够用的。
> - 要低 (caps): 肝 = AST / ALT / 胆红素、肾 = 肌酐、心电 = QT / QTc — 损伤/废物/危险信号，越少越好。
>
> 一层加深：白蛋白是肝"合成"的→要高；AST/ALT/胆红素是肝"损伤"的标志→要低。同是肝，合成高好、损伤低好。

## Relevant / exclusion conditions

No high/low direction here — **if present = excluded** (有则排除).

- **Cystitis, infective (感染性膀胱炎)** — bladder inflammation; relevant because the radioligand leaves the body through the urinary tract.
- **PSMA-targeted radioconjugate (PSMA靶向放射偶联物)** — the therapy itself: a ligand that binds PSMA, linked to a radioisotope (radioligand therapy); not a lab parameter. Same target as [[PSMA PET-CT]] (a theranostic pair).
- **Systemic anti-cancer therapy (全身性抗癌治疗)** — prior or concurrent systemic treatment; washout (洗脱期) & exclusion. See [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]].
- **Symptomatic (spinal) cord compression (症状性脊髓压迫)** — an oncologic emergency (肿瘤急症) from epidural (硬膜外) metastases; exclusion.
- **Hypersensitivity (超敏反应)** — allergy to the drug/excipients (辅料); exclusion. See [[Infusion-Related and Hypersensitivity Reactions]].
- **Xerostomia (口干)** — dry mouth. The salivary glands express PSMA, so PSMA radioligands collect in them off-target and harm them → xerostomia.
- **Cardiac disease (心脏疾病)** — e.g. recent MI (心肌梗死), heart failure (心衰), significant arrhythmia; exclusion (linked to QT/QTc above).
- **Thromboembolic or cerebrovascular events (血栓栓塞或脑血管事件)** — recent DVT (deep vein thrombosis, 深静脉血栓) / PE (pulmonary embolism, 肺栓塞) / stroke (卒中) / TIA (transient ischemic attack, 短暂性脑缺血发作); exclusion.
- **Malignancy (其他恶性肿瘤)** — a second/other primary cancer; excluded unless disease-free for a stated period.
- **Myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS, 骨髓增生异常综合征)** — a marrow disorder that can come before leukemia (pre-leukemic, 白血病前期); especially relevant for radioligand therapy, where radiation to the marrow risks secondary MDS/AML (acute myeloid leukemia, 急性髓系白血病).

## Relationship to prostate cancer

The labs themselves are **general oncology** safety parameters (used in nearly every trial), not specific to prostate. Their link to prostate cancer runs through two channels:

**Disease biology & population**

- **Metastasis mainly in bone → cancer growing into the marrow** (marrow infiltration, 骨髓浸润): anemia (Hgb↓), thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, less marrow reserve; spine metastases → symptomatic cord compression (a real complication of prostate cancer).
- **Elderly men with other diseases (comorbid, 合并症多), often on ADT**: cardiac disease, QT/QTc, and thromboembolic/cerebrovascular events are more common; albumin reflects performance status / prognosis.

**PSMA radioligand mechanism (the modality, used in mCRPC)**

- Leaves through the kidneys + the kidney limits the dose → **creatinine**.
- Salivary glands express PSMA → off-target uptake → **xerostomia** (signature toxicity, 标志性毒性).
- Excretion in urine + prostate and bladder are close together → **cystitis / bladder**.
- Marrow irradiation (bone mets gather the radioligand near the marrow) → **low blood counts (cytopenias) + secondary MDS/AML**.

Prostate-specific prior lines (ADT, ARPIs like abiraterone/enzalutamide, taxane chemo) define the washout for systemic anti-cancer therapy — see [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]].

**Bottom line:** general thresholds, but two things make them sharper in prostate cancer — a population that is bone-metastatic, elderly and has other diseases (marrow + cardiac + frailty 虚弱), and the PSMA-radioligand mechanism (kidney, salivary glands, bladder and marrow are exactly where the radiation lands).
