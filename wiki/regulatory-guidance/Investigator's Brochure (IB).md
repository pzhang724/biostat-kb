# Investigator's Brochure (IB)

The **Investigator's Brochure** (研究者手册) is the compiled body of all clinical + nonclinical data on the investigational product that is relevant to studying it in humans. Defined by **ICH E6 (GCP)**. It is the investigator's single reference document on the drug.

## What it's for

- Give investigators what they need to **understand and comply with the protocol** — rationale, dose, schedule, procedures.
- Support an informed **benefit-risk** (获益-风险) assessment for running the trial and treating subjects.
- Provide the **safety baseline** used during the trial.

## Typical contents

Physicochemical / pharmaceutical info · **nonclinical** data (pharmacology, toxicology, animal PK — see [[Pharmacokinetics (PK)]]) · **clinical** data so far (human PK/PD, safety, any efficacy) · a summary + guidance to the investigator.

## Why it matters for safety reporting

The IB carries the **Reference Safety Information (RSI)** — the list of **expected** serious adverse reactions. That list is the yardstick for "expected vs unexpected" when deciding whether an SAR is a [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)#SUSAR — the reportable subset|SUSAR]]. So the IB directly gates expedited reporting.

It is a **living document** — updated periodically (at least annually) as new data accrue. Once the drug is approved, the **label / SmPC** takes over the reference-safety role. A cross-cutting trial-conduct document in [[Oncology]].
