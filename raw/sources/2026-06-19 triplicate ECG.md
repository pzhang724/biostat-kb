Human asked: triplicate ECG 是什么意思?

Answer given (content marker):

Triplicate ECG = taking THREE 12-lead ECGs in quick succession at the same nominal timepoint (typically ~1–2 min apart, patient resting/supine), instead of a single recording. The QT/QTc (and other intervals) are then measured on all three and AVERAGED to get the value for that timepoint.

Why:
- QT/QTc has beat-to-beat and minute-to-minute biological variability + measurement noise. Averaging 3 reduces that variability → a more precise, reproducible QTc.
- This is the standard for cardiac-safety / QT assessment (ICH E14 thorough-QT studies and oncology protocols with QT concerns); often the protocol specifies triplicates at each scheduled ECG timepoint, frequently time-matched to PK sampling (so QTc can be related to drug concentration — exposure-response/C-QTc analysis).
- "Resting" + triplicate + standardized posture/timing = control the conditions so the averaged QTc is comparable across visits and patients.

One line: 同一时间点连做三次 12-lead ECG 取平均,降低 QT/QTc 的测量与生理变异,得到更可靠的数值;是 ICH E14 / QT 安全评估的标准做法,常和 PK 采血时间点对齐。
