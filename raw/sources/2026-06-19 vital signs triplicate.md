Q: Do vital signs also need triplicate?

A: Not routinely. Triplicate is canonical for ECG (QT/QTc has high beat-to-beat variability). For vital signs:
- BLOOD PRESSURE is the one vital sometimes measured in duplicate/triplicate (e.g. 3 readings after a rest period, averaged) to reduce variability — especially for drugs with cardiovascular/BP effects or when BP is a focus/endpoint.
- Other vitals (heart rate, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2) are usually SINGLE measurements.
- It is protocol-specified, driven by the parameter's importance and the drug's profile — not a universal requirement.

Data: each repeat is captured with a replicate number (VSREPNUM for VS, EGREPNUM for EG) and the average is derived in ADaM.
