# Patient Global Assessment (PGA)

A single-item patient-reported (患者报告) rating of **overall** treatment satisfaction / pain control — the patient's global impression of the analgesic in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- Typically a short categorical scale (e.g. poor / fair / good / excellent, or 0-4).
- Captures the patient's overall impression rather than a moment-by-moment pain score; complements the granular [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]].
- An investigator/clinician-rated counterpart (Clinician Global Assessment) sometimes runs alongside it.
