# Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)

**ICS** = inhaled corticosteroids (吸入性糖皮质激素) — the anti-inflammatory **controller / maintenance (控制/维持)** backbone (基础) of asthma treatment (budesonide 布地奈德, fluticasone 氟替卡松, beclomethasone 倍氯米松, etc.).

- They suppress (抑制) **type 2 / eosinophilic (嗜酸性) inflammation** in the airway → fewer symptoms, better FEV1 (forced expiratory volume in 1 second, 第一秒用力呼气容积), and fewer exacerbations (急性加重). The **inhaled route** gives a high dose in the airway with low exposure in the rest of the body (systemic exposure, 全身暴露), compared with oral corticosteroids (口服糖皮质激素, OCS).
- Often combined with a **LABA (long-acting β2-agonist, 长效β2受体激动剂)** as ICS/LABA. **High-dose ICS (± OCS)** defines the **severe-asthma** background, on top of which biologics (生物制剂) are added. The ICS dose / step is set by the stepwise (阶梯) framework of [[GINA (Global Initiative for Asthma)]].
- In trials: standard **background therapy** (背景治疗; patients stay on ICS) and a **severity / enrichment (富集)** criterion. High [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]] / eosinophils predict a good response to ICS.

**Direction and meaning**: ICS is a treatment, not a measured value, so there is no "good/bad" direction. But the **dose / step level shows severity**: needing **high-dose ICS (± OCS)** to stay controlled means **more severe, harder-to-control** asthma (the population where add-on biologics are studied).

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area; tied to [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]], [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]], and the [[Annualized Asthma Exacerbation Rate (AAER)]] endpoint.
