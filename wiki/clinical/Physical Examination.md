# Physical Examination

In a clinical trial the **physical examination (PE, 体格检查)** is an exam by a clinician, done **body system by body system** and recorded as **normal / abnormal** for each system. Systems in a **complete PE**:

- General appearance (一般情况)
- HEENT — head, eyes, ears, nose, throat (头颈五官)
- Skin (皮肤)
- Lymph nodes (淋巴结)
- Cardiovascular / heart (心血管)
- Respiratory / lungs (呼吸 / 肺)
- Abdomen (腹部)
- Musculoskeletal (肌肉骨骼)
- Neurological (神经系统)
- Extremities (四肢)

Usually done together with — but listed **separately** from — **vital signs** (生命体征: BP blood pressure 血压, HR heart rate 心率, temperature, respiratory rate 呼吸频率) and **height/weight**; sometimes also [[ECOG Performance Status]].

## Two flavors in the SoA

(SoA = Schedule of Assessments, 访视评估时间表.)

- **Complete / full PE** — at screening/baseline (筛选期/基线), and often at EOT (end of treatment, 治疗结束).
- **Symptom-directed / targeted PE** (针对性体检) — looks only at the relevant systems, at routine visits during treatment.

## Data-handling rule that matters

- An abnormality (异常) already present at **baseline** → recorded as **medical history** (既往病史).
- A **new or worsening** abnormality during treatment → recorded as an **adverse event (AE, 不良事件)**, graded by [[CTCAE Grade]].

So the PE is one of the sources that **creates AEs**. It is a row in the [[Schedule of Assessments]] and part of [[Oncology]] safety assessment, together with [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]].
