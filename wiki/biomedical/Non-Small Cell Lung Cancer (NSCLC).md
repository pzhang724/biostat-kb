# Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

The major class of lung cancer (**非小细胞肺癌**, ~85% of lung cancers), as opposed to **SCLC (small cell lung cancer, 小细胞肺癌)** — which is more aggressive / neuroendocrine and treated differently. A full trial-population descriptor like *"c-Met-overexpressing non-squamous NSCLC"* layers **class → histology → biomarker**.

## Histology: squamous vs non-squamous

NSCLC splits by **histology (组织学类型)**:
- **Squamous cell carcinoma (鳞状细胞癌)** — from squamous airway epithelium, more central, smoking-associated.
- **Non-squamous (非鳞)** — mainly **adenocarcinoma (腺癌)** (plus large cell).

The split is **treatment-relevant** (an eligibility axis, not just description): some drugs are indicated only in non-squamous (e.g. **pemetrexed**, **bevacizumab**), and most actionable **driver alterations (驱动基因变异)** — EGFR, ALK, ROS1, **MET** — are enriched in adenocarcinoma. So "non-squamous NSCLC" already defines a population.

## c-Met overexpression as a biomarker

**MET / c-Met** is a **receptor tyrosine kinase (受体酪氨酸激酶, RTK)** (ligand **HGF**). **c-Met overexpression (c-Met 过表达)** = high MET protein on the tumor cell, measured by **IHC (免疫组化)** — a **biomarker (生物标志物)** that defines a subset for **MET-targeted therapy**.

Distinct from other MET abnormalities:
- **MET overexpression** — protein level (IHC). ← this descriptor
- **MET amplification (基因扩增)** — gene copy-number gain.
- **MET exon 14 skipping (外显子14跳跃突变)** — a mutation.

c-Met-overexpressing non-squamous NSCLC is the **enrollment biomarker** for MET-directed agents — e.g. the antibody-drug conjugate **telisotuzumab vedotin (Teliso-V)**, EGFR-MET **bispecific antibodies** (e.g. amivantamab), and MET inhibitors. (Analogous to how [[PSMA PET-CT|PSMA]] expression selects patients for [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA in prostate cancer — a **target-selection biomarker**.)

**Putting the phrase together:** non-small-cell (not SCLC) · non-squamous histology (adenocarcinoma-type) · tumors overexpressing the c-Met protein — i.e. the patients a MET-targeted drug is tested in.

Part of [[Oncology]] (lung cancer indication). See [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]], [[RECIST 1.1]] (response criterion for NSCLC), [[PSMA PET-CT]] (parallel target-selection biomarker).
