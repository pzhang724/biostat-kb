# Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)

Area under the curve (曲线下面积) of pain intensity (疼痛强度) versus time, from 0 to 72 hours post-surgery/dose — the cumulative-pain-burden primary endpoint in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- Built from serial [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]] scores, integrated by the **trapezoidal rule** (梯形法则) over the fixed window.
- **Lower AUC = less total pain.** Captures the whole pain trajectory, not a single timepoint.
- **0-72h** chosen because acute post-surgical pain peaks in the first 2-3 days.
- Closely related to **SPID** (Sum of Pain Intensity Differences, 疼痛强度差值总和) — the time-weighted sum of changes from baseline.

Used as the primary efficacy measure in the [[Acute Pain]] thread; see also [[Schedule of Assessments]] for the timing of the pain assessments it integrates.
