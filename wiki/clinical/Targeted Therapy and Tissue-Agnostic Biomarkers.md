# Targeted Therapy and Tissue-Agnostic Biomarkers

Why a biomarker of target expression (靶点表达) can select patients across very different cancers (prostate, lung…).

## Same strategy ≠ same target

In [[Metastatic Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC)|prostate]] the selector (筛选标志) is [[PSMA PET-CT|PSMA]]; in [[Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)|lung]] it is **c-Met** — **two different proteins**. What they share is the **strategy (策略)**, not the molecule: pick patients by a **molecular/surface target (分子/表面靶点)** that the tumor has too much of (overexpresses, 过表达), then hit it with a **targeted drug (靶向药)** (e.g. an antibody-drug conjugate 抗体偶联药物, or a radioligand 放射性配体). Same playbook (套路), different target.

## The deeper truth: one target can span organs

In fact, a **single target is often shared across cancer types**, because cancers are more and more defined by their **molecular driver (驱动基因/分子驱动)**, not only by the organ where they started. The drug binds a **molecule** (a protein / mutation 突变 / fusion 融合); wherever that molecule is present — in any organ — the drug can act. So one target or drug can cover several tumors (e.g. **MET** in lung *and* gastric 胃 cancer; **HER2** in breast / gastric / lung).

## Basket trials and tissue-agnostic approvals

This is the basis of:

- **Basket trials (篮子试验)** — one biomarker, many tumor types enrolled together.
- **Tissue-agnostic approvals (组织不可知 / 泛瘤种获批)** — FDA approvals by **biomarker, whatever the tumor site**: e.g. **pembrolizumab** for **MSI-H/dMMR** (microsatellite instability-high / mismatch repair deficient, 微卫星高度不稳定/错配修复缺陷) solid tumors; **larotrectinib / entrectinib** for **NTRK-fusion** (NTRK 基因融合) solid tumors; **dabrafenib + trametinib** for **BRAF V600E** (BRAF V600E 突变) solid tumors.
- A **companion diagnostic (伴随诊断)** tests the biomarker to select patients.

**One line:** the organ of origin (原发器官) matters less and less than the molecular target — the *same strategy* across prostate and lung, and sometimes *literally the same target* across organs.
