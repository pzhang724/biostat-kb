Q: tumour 就是灶吗?(is "tumour" the same as 灶 / lesion?)

A:
- Not exactly equal — overlapping but different scope.
- 灶 / 病灶 / lesion = any LOCALIZED area of abnormal or diseased tissue. Broad: a lesion can be a tumor, but also inflammation, infection, a scar, a cyst, etc. It's a general "site of disease" word.
- 肿瘤 / tumor (neoplasm) = specifically an abnormal MASS/GROWTH of proliferating tissue. A tumor is one kind of lesion.
- Relationship: lesion ⊃ tumor (lesion is the broader term; every tumor is a lesion, not every lesion is a tumor).
- Why it feels interchangeable in oncology: in a cancer trial the lesions we track ARE tumors (primary tumor + metastatic tumors), so colloquially 病灶 ≈ 肿瘤 in that setting.
- But RECIST deliberately says "lesion", not "tumor", because some target lesions are things like lymph nodes — "lesion" is the precise, broader operative term.
