# Combining RECIST and PCWG3 into rPFS (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 combining RECIST and PCWG3 rPFS.md`. Follow-up to [[RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3 (chat)]].

- Question: how do RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3 combine into one decision?
- They govern different compartments and fold into the composite **rPFS**: soft tissue/nodes by [[RECIST 1.1]], bone by PCWG3 2+2.
- Logic = OR / earliest event: radiographic progression when either soft-tissue PD or bone 2+2 occurs first; death also an event.
- Bone date nuance: back-dated to the first scan showing the initial 2 new lesions, not the confirmation scan.
- PSA and symptoms stay separate (not folded into the radiographic call; no PD on PSA alone). OS is the ultimate endpoint.
