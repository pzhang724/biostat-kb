# Xerostomia

**Xerostomia (口干)** — dry mouth: there is less saliva (唾液) because the **salivary glands** (唾液腺) work less well.

With radioligand (放射性配体) drugs it is a **typical, common toxicity (毒性) of PSMA-targeted therapy** (PSMA = prostate-specific membrane antigen, 前列腺特异性膜抗原). The salivary glands **carry PSMA on their cells**, so [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|177Lu]]-PSMA or 225Ac-PSMA (actinium-225, 锕-225) collects there and irradiates (照射) the glands. The drug hits its real target, but in a tissue that is not the tumour (an off-target effect: on-target / off-tumour). It comes from where the tracer (示踪剂) binds (see [[PSMA PET-CT]], [[Dosimetry]]).

- Usually **worse and more likely to limit the dose** with alpha emitters (α 粒子发射体, e.g. 225Ac) than with 177Lu.
- Severity (严重程度) is graded by [[CTCAE Grade]]. It affects quality of life, eating and dental health (牙齿健康), and can lead to dose reduction or stopping treatment (discontinuation, 停药).

Listed as "prolonged xerostomia" (长期口干), an [[Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)]] in the radioligand trial. A toxicity of radioligand therapy in [[Oncology]].
