# PSA (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 PSA.md`.

- Question: what is PSA?
- PSA = prostate-specific antigen: glycoprotein from prostate epithelial cells, measured in blood.
- Most common prostate-cancer biomarker; used for screening/monitoring and as a trial efficacy/progression endpoint.
- Endpoints: PSA response (≥50% decline), PSA progression (PCWG3); used with imaging (RECIST, bone scan/PET).
- Connection: easily contaminated by concomitant/subsequent anti-cancer therapy.
