# Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

The main type of lung cancer (**非小细胞肺癌**, ~85% of lung cancers), as opposed to **SCLC (small cell lung cancer, 小细胞肺癌)**, which is more aggressive (侵袭性强) / neuroendocrine (神经内分泌) and is treated differently. A full description of a trial population, like *"c-Met-overexpressing non-squamous NSCLC"*, stacks **class → histology → biomarker**.

## Histology: squamous vs non-squamous

NSCLC is split by **histology (组织学类型)**:

- **Squamous cell carcinoma (鳞状细胞癌)** — starts from the squamous lining (epithelium, 上皮) of the airway; more central (中央型); linked to smoking.
- **Non-squamous (非鳞)** — mainly **adenocarcinoma (腺癌)** (plus large cell carcinoma, 大细胞癌).

The split **matters for treatment** (it is an eligibility axis, not just a description). Some drugs are approved (indicated, 适应证) only in non-squamous (e.g. **pemetrexed** 培美曲塞, **bevacizumab** 贝伐珠单抗), and most actionable (可用药的) **driver alterations (驱动基因变异)** — EGFR, ALK, ROS1, **MET** — are more common (enriched) in adenocarcinoma. So "non-squamous NSCLC" already defines a population.

## c-Met overexpression as a biomarker

**MET / c-Met** is a **receptor tyrosine kinase (受体酪氨酸激酶, RTK)**; its ligand (配体) is **HGF** (hepatocyte growth factor, 肝细胞生长因子). **c-Met overexpression (c-Met 过表达)** = a lot of MET protein on the tumor cell, measured by **IHC (immunohistochemistry, 免疫组化)** — a **biomarker (生物标志物)** that defines a subset for **MET-targeted therapy**.

It is different from other MET abnormalities:

- **MET overexpression** — protein level (IHC). ← this descriptor
- **MET amplification (基因扩增)** — more copies of the gene (copy-number gain, 拷贝数增加).
- **MET exon 14 skipping (外显子14跳跃突变)** — a mutation.

c-Met-overexpressing non-squamous NSCLC is the **enrollment biomarker** for drugs aimed at MET — e.g. the antibody-drug conjugate (抗体偶联药物) **telisotuzumab vedotin (Teliso-V)**, EGFR-MET **bispecific antibodies** (双特异性抗体; e.g. amivantamab), and MET inhibitors (抑制剂). (This is like how [[PSMA PET-CT|PSMA]] expression selects patients for [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA in prostate cancer — a **target-selection biomarker**.)

**Putting the phrase together:** non-small-cell (not SCLC) · non-squamous histology (adenocarcinoma type) · tumors with too much c-Met protein — i.e. the patients a MET-targeted drug is tested in.
