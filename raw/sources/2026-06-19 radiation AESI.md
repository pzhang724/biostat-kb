AESI = Adverse Event of Special Interest (特别关注的不良事件). A PRE-SPECIFIED adverse event (or group of events) that the sponsor flags for proactive, closer surveillance — because of a known/suspected link to the drug or its class, a regulatory concern, or a class-effect signal worth characterizing. Key point: an AESI gets special handling EVEN IF it is not "serious" by the SAE definition.

How an AESI differs from ordinary AE / SAE:
- Ordinary AE: collected and graded (CTCAE), no special trigger.
- SAE: seriousness-based (death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, etc.) → expedited reporting.
- AESI: interest-based, pre-defined in the protocol. Often comes with extra data collection (a targeted questionnaire / extra labs / follow-up), sometimes expedited or accelerated reporting to the sponsor even if non-serious, and dedicated safety summaries. It is about CHARACTERIZING a specific risk, not just severity.

"Radiation AESI" = the AESIs tied to the radioligand's radiation mechanism — they cluster at the organs where the radioligand deposits dose. The protocol's actual list (shared by human):
- Prolonged myelosuppression (持续/延长的骨髓抑制) — red-marrow irradiation; "prolonged" = the special interest is the persistent, slow-to-recover cytopenia, not transient dips.
- Nephrotoxicity (肾毒性) — kidney is dose-limiting (renal excretion).
- Leukemia and myelodysplastic syndromes (白血病和骨髓增生异常综合征, MDS/AML) — long-latency radiation-induced marrow malignancy; flagship long-term AESI for radioligand therapy.
- Other secondary malignancies (其他继发性恶性肿瘤) — any other radiation-induced second primary cancer.
- Prolonged xerostomia (持续口干) — salivary glands express PSMA → off-target irradiation; "prolonged" = persistent dry mouth.
- Hyperthyroidism / hypothyroidism (甲亢/甲减) — thyroid dysfunction; this is why TSH is on the chemistry panel.

Note the "prolonged" qualifier and the secondary-malignancy / thyroid entries: the special interest is the PERSISTENT and LONG-LATENCY harms of the radiation mechanism — exactly the things that need committed up-front surveillance, extra data, and long-term follow-up to characterize, beyond ordinary acute AE grading.
