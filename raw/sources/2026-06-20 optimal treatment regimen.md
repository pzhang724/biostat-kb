Q: 什么是 optimal treatment regimen

A: A treatment regimen is the complete "how" of giving a therapy: which drug(s), the
dose, route, schedule/frequency, cycle length, total duration, and any combination or
sequence — not a single number, the whole plan. An "optimal" treatment regimen is the
regimen that best balances efficacy and safety/tolerability (best benefit-risk), which is
not necessarily the maximum tolerated dose.

This is the central idea of FDA's Project Optimus: move away from the old "MTD = more is
better" mindset toward deliberately selecting an optimal dose AND schedule before the
pivotal trial. Ideally the RP2D should be the optimal regimen, not just the toxicity
ceiling.

In practice the optimal regimen is identified by comparing multiple dose/schedule arms on
efficacy, PK/PD and exposure-response, safety, long-term tolerability over many cycles,
and sometimes patient-reported outcomes.
