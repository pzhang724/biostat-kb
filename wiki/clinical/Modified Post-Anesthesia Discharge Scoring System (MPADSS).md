# Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System (MPADSS)

A standard scoring rule that decides when a patient is ready to go home (**discharge**, 出院) after anesthesia (麻醉) for day surgery (ambulatory surgery, 日间手术). Acute pain (急性疼痛) trials use it to measure recovery.

Five criteria, each scored 0–2 (total 0–10):

- **Vital signs** (生命体征)
- **Ambulation** (行走能力)
- **Nausea/vomiting** (恶心/呕吐)
- **Pain** (疼痛)
- **Surgical bleeding** (手术出血)

A total of **9 or more** usually means the patient is ready for discharge. Part of the [[Acute Pain]] thread, together with the day-surgery setting in [[Osteotomy and Bunionectomy (Acute Pain Surgical Model)]].
