Q: physical examination 包括什么？

A (summary delivered in chat):

In a clinical trial the physical examination (PE, 体格检查) is a by-body-system clinician exam,
recorded as normal/abnormal per system. Typical systems covered in a COMPLETE PE:

- General appearance (一般情况)
- HEENT — head, eyes, ears, nose, throat (头颈五官)
- Skin (皮肤)
- Lymph nodes (淋巴结)
- Cardiovascular / heart (心血管)
- Respiratory / lungs (呼吸/肺部)
- Abdomen (腹部)
- Musculoskeletal (肌肉骨骼)
- Neurological (神经系统)
- Extremities (四肢)

Usually paired with — but listed separately from — vital signs (BP, HR, temperature, respiratory
rate) and height/weight; sometimes ECOG performance status.

Two flavors in the SoA:
- Complete / full PE — at screening/baseline (and often EOT).
- Symptom-directed / targeted PE — focused on relevant systems at routine on-treatment visits.

Data-handling rule that matters: an abnormality present at BASELINE → recorded as medical history;
a NEW or worsening abnormality on-treatment → recorded as an adverse event (AE). So the PE is one
of the inputs that generates AEs.
