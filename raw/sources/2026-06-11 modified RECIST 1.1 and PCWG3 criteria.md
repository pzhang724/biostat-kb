Correction from human: this is ONE criterion, not two bolted together. Full protocol name:
"Modified Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors Criteria (Version 1.1) and Prostate Cancer Working Group 3 Criteria" — commonly written "modified RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3 criteria".

What it is:
- A single, named composite response/progression-assessment criterion used in advanced/mCRPC prostate-cancer trials. It is the protocol-specified standard that defines radiographic assessments (and thus rPFS).
- "Modified RECIST 1.1" = RECIST 1.1 applied to soft-tissue/nodal disease with prostate-specific adaptations (bone disease sits outside the measurable-target framework; nodal/new-lesion handling adapted), integrated WITH PCWG3's bone-scan rules — together as one criterion.
- Component standards: [[RECIST 1.1]] (soft tissue/nodes) + PCWG3 (bone scan 2+2, PSA, philosophy). The composite is the thing protocols actually cite.

How it decides (the single criterion's logic):
- Response (CR/PR/SD): from the modified-RECIST soft-tissue assessment (bone can't yield response).
- Progression (radiographic): union/OR — soft-tissue RECIST PD OR bone 2+2 confirmed OR death = rPFS event; earliest date; bone back-dated to first scan with the initial 2 new lesions.
- Unconfirmed ≥2 new bone lesions = not PD yet (await confirmatory scan).
- CR requires zero bone disease.
- PSA and symptoms are tracked separately, NOT inputs to this radiographic criterion.
- (Fused soft-tissue × bone matrix recorded on the concept page.)

Primary use: defines rPFS (the composite radiographic progression endpoint) in mCRPC trials; OS is the ultimate endpoint.
