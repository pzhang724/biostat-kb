# Osteotomy and Bunionectomy (Acute Pain Surgical Model)

- **Osteotomy** (截骨术) — surgery that cuts a bone to put it back in the right line (realign, 矫正对线).
- **Bunionectomy** (拇外翻矫正术) — fixing a bunion (拇囊炎/拇外翻) by an osteotomy of the **first metatarsal** (第一跖骨). It is one of the **FDA-standard surgical models for acute pain** (together with third molar extraction, 第三磨牙拔除, and abdominoplasty, 腹壁整形术). It is chosen because the pain after surgery is moderate to severe, and it is predictable and reproducible (可重复的).
- **Unilateral** (单侧) — done on one foot only, so surgery on the other foot (contralateral, 对侧) does not confound (混杂) the pain signal.

The pain it causes is measured by the [[Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)]] and [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]] endpoints; recovery and discharge (出院) by the [[Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System (MPADSS)]]. It is the base of the [[Acute Pain]] thread.
