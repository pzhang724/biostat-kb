# Infusion-Related and Hypersensitivity Reactions

A **safety section** of the protocol that covers two overlapping groups of **acute (急性) reactions** to a product given by infusion or injection.

- **Infusion-related reaction (IRR, 输注相关反应)** — symptoms **during or shortly after** the IV (intravenous, 静脉) infusion: fever, chills/rigors (寒战), flushing (潮红), low or high blood pressure (hypo-/hypertension), shortness of breath (dyspnea, 呼吸困难), nausea, back pain. Often **linked to the infusion rate / driven by cytokines (细胞因子)**, *not* a true allergy. Managed by **slowing or pausing** the infusion ± premedication.
- **Hypersensitivity reaction (HSR, 超敏/过敏反应)** — an **allergic reaction caused by the immune system** (immune-mediated, 免疫介导) to the drug or to an excipient (辅料). Ranges from mild (rash 皮疹, urticaria 荨麻疹, itching 瘙痒) up to **anaphylaxis** (过敏性休克 — bronchospasm 支气管痉挛, angioedema 血管性水肿, hypotension, shock 休克). Can be IgE-mediated (immunoglobulin E, 免疫球蛋白 E; a true allergy) or not.

## Why grouped

At the bedside the two **look alike** and are hard to tell apart in the moment, so protocols handle them together. The section usually specifies:

- how to recognize them + **monitoring** during/after the infusion;
- **grading** by [[CTCAE Grade]];
- step-by-step **management** — stop/slow the infusion; antihistamines (抗组胺药), corticosteroids; **epinephrine** (肾上腺素) + emergency care for anaphylaxis;
- any **premedication** (see [[Anti-Emetic Premedication]] for the related supportive-care idea).

Known (pre-existing, 既往) **hypersensitivity** to the drug or its excipients is usually an **exclusion criterion** (排除标准) — see [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]]. A safety concept in [[Oncology]].
