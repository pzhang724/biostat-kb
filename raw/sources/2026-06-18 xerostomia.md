Q: Xerostomia 是什么？

A (summary delivered in chat):

Xerostomia (口干) = dry mouth — the symptom of reduced saliva from impaired salivary gland
function. In the radioligand context it's a hallmark / common toxicity of PSMA-targeted therapy:
salivary glands express PSMA, so 177Lu-/225Ac-PSMA accumulates there and irradiates the glands
(an off-target / on-target-off-tumour effect). Tends to be worse and more dose-limiting with
alpha emitters (225Ac) than with 177Lu. Severity graded by CTCAE; can affect quality of life,
eating, dental health, and sometimes drives dose reduction/discontinuation.
