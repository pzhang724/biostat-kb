# Combined radiographic assessment matrix (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 combined radiographic assessment matrix.md`. Clarification: human wanted the FUSED single decision, not the two standards side by side.

- Key asymmetry: bone scan contributes **progression only**, never response (no CR/PR off bone). So response = soft-tissue RECIST; progression = union of soft tissue OR bone.
- Single rule: overall = PD if soft-tissue RECIST PD **or** bone confirmed 2+2 **or** death; else take the soft-tissue RECIST category, with CR requiring zero bone disease.
- Unconfirmed ≥2 new bone lesions = not PD yet (defer to confirmatory scan).
- Full fused matrix (soft-tissue RECIST × bone 2+2 status) recorded on [[PCWG3 Criteria]].
- PSA & symptoms are not inputs to the radiographic call.
