# Best overall response (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 best overall response.md`. Human's insight in the RECIST/PCWG3 thread.

- Human's point: however complex the criterion, at a single timepoint there is ONE response status; the best is the best among all of them.
- Confirmed — two levels: timepoint response (CR/PR/SD/PD/NE per visit) → Best Overall Response (best across all visits, CR>PR>SD>PD).
- Constraints on the max: CR/PR confirmation (≥4 wk), minimum SD duration, assessed only up to first PD, NE if no evaluable post-baseline scan.
- Downstream: ORR = BOR of CR/PR; DCR = BOR of CR/PR/SD.
