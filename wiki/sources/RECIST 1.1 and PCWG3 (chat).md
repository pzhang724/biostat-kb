# RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3 (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3.md`. Follow-up in the PSA / prostate-cancer thread.

- Question: explain RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3.
- RECIST 1.1: imaging response rules. Measurable ≥10 mm (nodes short axis ≥15 mm); ≤5 target lesions (≤2/organ) → sum of diameters. CR / PR (≥30%↓) / PD (≥20%↑ from nadir +≥5 mm, or new lesion) / SD. CR/PR confirmed in ORR trials.
- PCWG3: mCRPC trial criteria where RECIST can't handle bone disease. Bone "2+2 rule" (2 new lesions, confirmed by 2 more) to filter bone-scan flare; soft tissue by RECIST 1.1; PSA progression = ≥25% & ≥2 ng/mL above nadir, confirmed. Philosophy: "still benefiting?" vs first change; report progression by mode; preferred endpoints rPFS + OS.
