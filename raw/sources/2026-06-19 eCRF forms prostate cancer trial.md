Q: For a prostate cancer trial, walk through what eCRF forms are needed.

Grouped by timing; SDTM domain in (). Prostate = male, so no subject pregnancy test (partner-pregnancy form may still exist). Central lab / central imaging often arrive as non-CRF feeds, not keyed on the eCRF.

SCREENING / BASELINE
- Informed Consent (consent date)
- Inclusion/Exclusion — Eligibility (IE)
- Demographics (DM)
- Medical History (MH)
- Disease History / Characteristics — initial diagnosis, Gleason score, TNM stage, metastatic sites, castration status
- Prior anti-cancer therapy — surgery / radiotherapy / systemic (PR / CM)
- Prior & Concomitant Medications (CM)
- Vital Signs (VS), Height/Weight, Physical Exam (PE)
- ECOG Performance Status (QS/RS)
- 12-lead ECG (EG)
- Local labs: Hematology / Chemistry / Coagulation / Urinalysis (LB)
- PSA (LB) and Testosterone (LB, to confirm castrate level)
- Tumour assessment baseline: lesion identification (TU) + measurements (TR) + response (RS); CT/MRI soft tissue + bone scan, +/- PSMA PET-CT

TREATMENT (each cycle/visit)
- Study Drug Administration / Dosing / Exposure (EX) incl. dose modifications
- Drug Accountability (often IRT/RTSM, not CRF)
- Adverse Events (AE); Concomitant Medications (CM)
- Vital Signs / targeted PE / ECOG; periodic ECG; labs + PSA (LB)
- Tumour assessment at scheduled imaging visits (TU/TR/RS)
- PK sampling (PC/PP) if applicable (e.g. radioligand)
- PRO / QoL: FACT-P, Brief Pain Inventory (BPI), EQ-5D, analgesic/opioid use (QS)

END OF TREATMENT
- EOT form: date + reason for treatment discontinuation
- Final tumour / labs / PE / ECG

FOLLOW-UP
- Safety follow-up (AE/SAE + con-med continuation)
- Disease/progression follow-up (if PFS followed off-treatment)
- Survival follow-up = OS status + subsequent anti-cancer therapy

TRIGGERED / AS-NEEDED
- Serious Adverse Event (SAE) report
- Death form (date + cause)
- Skeletal-related / symptomatic skeletal event (SSE/SRE) — prostate bone mets
- New anti-cancer therapy
- Unscheduled visit
- Protocol deviation
- Partner pregnancy (if applicable)

Prostate-specific emphasis: PSA + testosterone (castrate confirm); bone scan (PCWG3 2+2) fused with soft-tissue RECIST into the composite criterion; pain/opioid + SSE; FACT-P/BPI PROs.
