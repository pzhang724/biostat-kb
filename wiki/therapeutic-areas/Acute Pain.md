# Acute Pain

The main narrative for the learning thread on acute (post-surgical) pain (急性/术后疼痛) — it strings together how the efficacy of pain drugs (analgesics, 镇痛药) is measured in acute pain trials, and the surgical model that creates the pain.

## The surgical model

Acute pain trials need a pain stimulus (刺激) that can be reproduced. The standard model here is the **bunionectomy** (拇外翻矫正术) — an [[Osteotomy and Bunionectomy (Acute Pain Surgical Model)]] — done on one side only (**unilateral**, 单侧: one foot), so that a second surgical site does not confound (混杂) the pain signal.

## Measuring efficacy

- **Total (cumulative) pain** — the primary endpoint is the [[Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)]] (area under the pain-intensity curve over the first 72 h).
- **Pain intensity instrument** (量表) — that AUC is built from repeated [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]] scores.
- **Opioid-sparing** (阿片节约) — the [[Opioid-Free Rate]] (and total opioid use / time to the first rescue).
- **Patient impression** — [[Patient Global Assessment (PGA)]].
- **Readiness for discharge** (出院) — the [[Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System (MPADSS)]] for recovery after day surgery.

## Analysis & missing data

The continuous pain endpoint is usually analysed with [[ANCOVA (Analysis of Covariance)]], adjusting for baseline pain. Missing scores (caused by [[Prohibited and Rescue Medication|rescue medication]] and [[Withdrawal due to AE or Lack of Efficacy]]) are filled in by [[Imputation and Carried-Forward Methods (LOCF, WOCF, wWOCF)]] — this choice puts the [[Estimand]]'s intercurrent-event strategy into practice.

## Safety

When a local anesthetic (局部麻醉药) is used for pain control after surgery, the headline safety event is [[Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity (LAST)]]. The surgical cut (incision, 切口) is followed for [[Wound Healing]] — the pain drug must not harm it.
