From chat, 2026-06-19 (follow-up to SAE criteria)

Q: Relationship between SUSAR and SAE.

A: SUSAR = Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction. It is a **subset of SAE** produced by adding two more filters on top of "serious":

Nesting: AE ⊃ SAE ⊃ SAR ⊃ SUSAR.

1. Start: an SAE (a serious AE — meets one of the 6 seriousness criteria).
2. + **Suspected / Related** — causality: at least a reasonable possibility the study drug caused it. An SAE that is suspected-related = SAR (Serious Adverse Reaction). SAEs judged unrelated are not SARs.
3. + **Unexpected** — its nature, severity, or frequency is NOT already documented in the Reference Safety Information (RSI), i.e. the Investigator's Brochure (for an IMP) or the SmPC/label. Already-listed reactions are "expected".

So SUSAR = an SAE that is BOTH suspected-related AND unexpected.

Why it matters: SUSARs trigger **expedited regulatory reporting** to authorities and ethics committees — typically 7 calendar days for fatal/life-threatening, 15 days for other SUSARs. A plain SAE that is expected or unrelated does not get this expedited single-case channel (it's still recorded and reported in aggregate, e.g. DSUR).

Relationship to the regulatory authority (the reporting flow):
- **Investigator → Sponsor:** the site reports every SAE to the sponsor promptly (typically within 24 hours). The investigator gives a causality opinion; the sponsor does NOT just file SAEs one-by-one to the authority.
- **Sponsor evaluates** each SAE for causality + expectedness (against the RSI/IB). Only those that come out SUSAR get the single-case expedited channel.
- **Sponsor → Regulatory authority (+ ethics committees/IRBs + all participating investigators):** SUSARs are reported expedited — 7 days fatal/life-threatening, 15 days other. In the US these are **IND safety reports** (21 CFR 312.32); in the EU they go to authorities via **EudraVigilance (EVCTM)**.
- **Aggregate / periodic:** all SAEs (and the wider safety picture) roll up into the annual **DSUR** (Development Safety Update Report) to authorities — so non-SUSAR SAEs are still reported, just periodically rather than expedited.

One line: the **sponsor** is the hub — sites send SAEs in; the sponsor filters to SUSARs and pushes those to the authority expedited, everything else aggregated in the DSUR.
