# Anti-Emetic Premedication

An **anti-emetic (止吐) premedication** (预防性用药) regimen (方案) — drugs given **beforehand, to prevent** (prophylactically, 预防性) **nausea and vomiting** (恶心呕吐) around an infusion (输注). A typical protocol line: *"dexamethasone and domperidone/metoclopramide or institutional anti-emetic regimen."*

- **Dexamethasone (地塞米松)** — a corticosteroid (糖皮质激素) used as an anti-emetic (and anti-inflammatory, 抗炎) premedication.
- **Domperidone (多潘立酮) / Metoclopramide (甲氧氯普胺)** — prokinetic (促胃动力) **dopamine D2-receptor antagonists** (多巴胺 D2 受体拮抗剂), classic anti-emetics. The "/" means either one (often chosen by what the site has; domperidone gets into the CNS, central nervous system 中枢神经系统, less; metoclopramide is more widely available).
- **"or institutional anti-emetic regimen"** — the site may use its own hospital-standard anti-emetic protocol instead. This gives flexibility, and still makes sure the prevention happens.

## Why here

In [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|177Lu]]-PSMA radioligand therapy, the dose is **infused together with amino acids** (氨基酸; arginine 精氨酸 / lysine 赖氨酸) to **protect the kidneys** (renal protection, 肾保护). These amino acids very often cause nausea/vomiting, so anti-emetic premedication is standard supportive care (支持治疗). It is prevention, not treatment after the fact.
