# PSMA PET-CT

**PSMA = Prostate-Specific Membrane Antigen (前列腺特异性膜抗原)** — a protein found in large amounts on the **surface** of most prostate-cancer cells. (Different from [[PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)|PSA]], which is an antigen *released* into the blood and measured there; PSMA is a target on the cell *membrane* (膜) that you image.)

**PSMA PET/CT** is a molecular imaging scan: a radioactive tracer (示踪剂) that binds PSMA is injected; a **[[PET (Positron Emission Tomography)|PET]]** scanner detects where it collects (i.e. where the prostate-cancer cells are); and **CT** gives the anatomical map — function + anatomy, combined (fused, 融合). Common tracers: **⁶⁸Ga-PSMA-11, ¹⁸F-DCFPyL (piflufolastat), ¹⁸F-PSMA-1007**.

**Uses:**

- Staging (分期) of higher-risk prostate cancer.
- Finding / locating **biochemical recurrence** (生化复发; rising PSA after treatment) — it finds disease at much lower PSA levels than conventional imaging.
- Much more **sensitive** (敏感) than conventional imaging (CT + technetium 锝 bone scan): it finds smaller lesions in nodes / bone / organs (visceral, 内脏), and earlier.
- **Theranostics** (诊疗一体化) — the same PSMA target is used for *therapy* (e.g. [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA-617 radioligand therapy). Patients are often selected by a positive PSMA-PET, and the radiation delivered is measured by [[Dosimetry]].

**The scan, step by step:** inject a radiolabeled PSMA ligand → **wait for uptake (~60 min)** while it binds PSMA on the surface of cancer cells and the unbound tracer clears → [[PET (Positron Emission Tomography)|PET]] detects the photons from positron annihilation (coincidence) → CT gives the anatomy; the images are fused, and uptake is measured as **SUV**. Disease that expresses PSMA (primary, nodes, bone, visceral) "lights up". Confirming that the tumour expresses PSMA is exactly the selection step before [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA therapy.

**What high vs low PSMA means:** "high/low PSMA" = how much of the **target** the tumour shows, read from the scan as **uptake / SUV**.

- **High PSMA** (high uptake) = the lesion **strongly expresses** PSMA → **lights up** brightly → lots of target.
- **Low / negative PSMA** = little or no expression → **doesn't light up**.

Why it matters: (1) **diagnostic** — high uptake = PSMA-avid (PSMA 高摄取) disease; it confirms/detects lesions; (2) **therapy selection** — PSMA is the **target** for [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|¹⁷⁷Lu]]-PSMA therapy, so **high PSMA → more target → more dose delivered → a good candidate, more likely to respond** (PSMA-PET is the eligibility scan; you need enough uptake to qualify), while **low/negative → too little target → a poor candidate / excluded**, and such disease can progress without lighting up; (3) **biology** — expression is higher in higher-grade / advanced disease but is **heterogeneous** (异质性的; one patient can have both PSMA+ and PSMA− lesions). One line: **high = lots of target** (avid + good for radioligand), **low = little target** (doesn't light up + poor radioligand candidate).

**Specificity caveats (特异性方面的注意事项):** PSMA is **not 100% specific to the prostate** — there is normal (physiologic, 生理性) uptake in the salivary/lacrimal (泪腺) glands, kidneys, liver, bowel (肠道), and some benign (良性) / other tumours → possible **false positives** (假阳性; needs an expert read). And **not all prostate cancer is PSMA-positive** (PSMA-low/negative disease exists), so a negative scan does not fully rule it out.

**Caveat vs response criteria:** [[RECIST 1.1]] / [[PCWG3 Criteria]] (and the composite [[Modified RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3 Criteria]]) were built on **conventional** imaging. Because PSMA PET is more sensitive, it causes stage migration (分期迁移) and can show progression earlier or in a different way (discordantly, 不一致地). How to fold PSMA-PET findings into formal response/progression endpoints is still **evolving** (emerging PSMA-PET response criteria such as RECIP). So PSMA PET is excellent for **detection/selection**, but is not yet the standard **response-assessment** criterion. See [[Measurable vs Non-Measurable Disease (RECIST)]].
