# Acute Pain

Main narrative for the acute (post-surgical) pain (急性疼痛) learning thread — strings together how analgesic efficacy is measured in acute pain trials and the surgical model that generates the pain.

## The surgical model

Acute pain trials need a reproducible pain stimulus. The standard model here is the **bunionectomy** (拇外翻矫正术) — a [[Osteotomy and Bunionectomy (Acute Pain Surgical Model)]] — done **unilateral** (单侧, one foot) so the pain signal isn't confounded by a second site.

## Measuring efficacy

- **Cumulative pain** — the primary endpoint is the [[Pain Intensity AUC (0-72h)]] (area under the pain-intensity curve over the first 72h).
- **Pain intensity instrument** — that AUC is built from serial [[Pain NRS at Rest and with Movement (NRS-R, NRS-M)]] scores.
- **Opioid-sparing** — the [[Opioid-Free Rate]] (and total opioid use / time to first rescue).
- **Patient impression** — [[Patient Global Assessment (PGA)]].
- **Discharge readiness** — the [[Modified Post-Anesthesia Discharge Scoring System (MPADSS)]] for day-surgery recovery.

## Analysis & missing data

The continuous pain endpoint is usually analysed by [[ANCOVA (Analysis of Covariance)]] adjusting for baseline pain. Missing scores (driven by rescue medication and [[Withdrawal due to AE or Lack of Efficacy]]) are filled by [[Imputation and Carried-Forward Methods (LOCF, WOCF, wWOCF)]] — the choice operationalizes the [[Estimand]]'s intercurrent-event strategy.
