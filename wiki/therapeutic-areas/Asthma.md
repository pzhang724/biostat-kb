# Asthma

**Asthma (哮喘)** — a long-term (chronic) inflammatory disease of the airways (气道) with airflow obstruction that can be reversed (reversible obstruction): the airways tighten (bronchoconstriction, 支气管收缩) and open again with bronchodilators (支气管扩张剂). It is often linked to allergy/atopy (特应性) and usually starts in childhood. The key feature is obstruction that varies and can be reversed — compare [[COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)]], where the obstruction is mostly not reversible.

The main narrative for the asthma learning thread — it strings together how disease activity is measured in asthma trials.

## Measuring disease

Disease activity in asthma trials is read on four fronts:

- **Lung function** by spirometry (肺量计) — [[Pre-BD and Post-BD FEV1]] (the change from pre- to post-bronchodilator = reversibility, 可逆性) and its partner [[Forced Vital Capacity (FVC)]] (the FEV1/FVC ratio = obstruction).
- **Inflammation biomarkers** of the type 2 / eosinophilic (嗜酸性) phenotype — the breath marker [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]] and the blood-based [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]] (eosinophils, IL-5, IgE, TARC).
- **Clinical efficacy** — the [[Annualized Asthma Exacerbation Rate (AAER)]] (the usual primary endpoint).
- **Patient experience** — [[Asthma Patient-Reported Outcomes (Symptom Diary, ACQ-6, AQLQ, EQ-5D)]].

The controller backbone (控制治疗的基础) is [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]], on top of which type 2 biologics (生物制剂) are added. Most of these biomarkers and the biologic strategy fit together as the [[T2-High Asthma]] endotype (内型). The overall management framework — control assessment, severity, and the treatment steps — is the [[GINA (Global Initiative for Asthma)]] strategy.

## Treatment

It treats the inflammation first: ICS is the controller backbone; a long-acting bronchodilator (LABA, long-acting β2-agonist, 长效β2受体激动剂) is added for control, and a short-acting bronchodilator (SABA, short-acting β2-agonist, 短效β2受体激动剂) is the rescue (缓解) inhaler for sudden symptoms; treatment is stepped up/down per GINA. Severe [[T2-High Asthma]] can add biologics (anti-IgE, anti-IL5, etc.), guided by [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]].

Contrast with [[COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)]]: asthma = anti-inflammatory treatment (ICS) first, with a bronchodilator as reliever/add-on; COPD = bronchodilator first, ICS only as an add-on for some patients, plus a big non-drug part (smoking cessation, 戒烟) that asthma does not stress in the same way.
