# Prostate Cancer

Main-narrative map for the prostate-cancer (within oncology) learning thread. It strings together the terms learned so far, each linked to its concept-type page.

## The thread

How treatment effect is read out in advanced/mCRPC trials: a [[PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)|PSA]] biomarker and [[PSMA PET-CT]] imaging measure disease; tumor response is assessed by [[RECIST 1.1]] for soft tissue and [[PCWG3 Criteria]] for bone, fused into the [[Modified RECIST 1.1 and PCWG3 Criteria]] criterion (constrained by [[Measurable vs Non-Measurable Disease (RECIST)|measurable disease]]); per-timepoint calls roll up to a [[Best Overall Response]] and downstream endpoints; [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials|other anti-cancer therapy]] confounds the read, [[PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen)|PSA]] is only a [[Surrogate Endpoint]], subsequent therapy is an [[Intercurrent Event]], and radioligand programs add [[Pharmacokinetics (PK)]] / [[Dosimetry]].
