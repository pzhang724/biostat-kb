# IRB and IEC (Ethics Committees)

**IRB** (Institutional Review Board 机构审查委员会) and **IEC** (Independent Ethics Committee 独立伦理委员会) are the **same function under two names** — the independent committee that protects the **rights, safety, and well-being** of human trial subjects. **IRB** = US term (FDA **21 CFR 56**); **IEC** = the **ICH / EU** term ([[Investigator's Brochure (IB)|ICH E6 GCP]]).

## What they do

- **Before start** — review and **approve** the protocol, the **informed consent form (ICF)**, the [[Investigator's Brochure (IB)]], recruitment materials, and investigator qualifications; weigh **risk-benefit**; ensure informed consent is adequate.
- **Ongoing** — **continuing review** (≥ annually), review of amendments, receipt of **safety reports** ([[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)#SUSAR — the reportable subset|SUSARs]] / expedited) and progress reports; can **suspend or terminate** approval.
- **Composition** — independent members, including **scientific and non-scientific / lay** community members.

## Two separate gates

A site **cannot enroll** without ethics approval. **Ethics approval (IRB/IEC)** and **regulatory authorization** (the authority, e.g. FDA **IND**) are **two separate gates — both required**. The ethics committee is also one of the recipients in the SUSAR [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)#Reporting flow to the regulatory authority|reporting flow]].

## CIOMS (related)

**CIOMS** (Council for International Organizations of Medical Sciences) is an international NGO (under WHO/UNESCO) — **not** an oversight committee. In drug safety it means the **CIOMS Form** (CIOMS I), the de-facto international standard **paper form for an expedited individual case safety report (ICSR)** — the human-readable analog of an **ICH E2B** electronic report, used to transmit SUSARs/SAEs to authorities and ethics committees. CIOMS working groups also authored foundational **pharmacovigilance** guidance (CIOMS I–VIII) and international **ethics** guidelines for health-related research. So CIOMS is about **how a SUSAR/SAE is transmitted**, distinct from the IRB/IEC ethics gate. A cross-cutting trial-conduct concept in [[Oncology]].
