# Withdrawal due to AE or Lack of Efficacy

Two key reasons a subject discontinues treatment early — both are [[Intercurrent Event]]s, but they are handled differently:

- **Lack of efficacy** (疗效不足) — the drug isn't controlling pain. Conservative imputation (WOCF / BOCF) assumes continued / worst pain, i.e. the patient is counted as a failure.
- **Adverse event** (不良事件, AE) — withdrawal driven by toxicity, not pain. The pain at withdrawal may have been fine, so handling differs from a lack-of-efficacy dropout.

These map onto the [[Estimand]] ICE strategies — e.g. **composite** (count the ICE as failure), **treatment-policy**, **hypothetical**. The handling choice is then operationalized by an [[Imputation and Carried-Forward Methods (LOCF, WOCF, wWOCF)]] rule. Part of the [[Acute Pain]] thread.
