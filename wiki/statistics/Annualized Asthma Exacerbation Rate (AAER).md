# Annualized Asthma Exacerbation Rate (AAER)

**AAER** = the number of **asthma exacerbations (哮喘急性加重)** per patient per year — the standard **primary efficacy endpoint** in asthma trials, especially for biologics (生物制剂) in severe asthma.

- **Exacerbation** = a worsening, defined in the protocol, that needs **systemic / oral corticosteroids (全身/口服糖皮质激素)** (usually ≥3 days), or an emergency-department visit / hospitalization.
- **"Annualized" (年化)** = total exacerbations ÷ total follow-up time, scaled to a per-year rate, so patients with different exposure times can be compared.
- Analyzed with **negative binomial regression (负二项回归)** — a count outcome with over-dispersion (过离散; more spread than a Poisson model expects), using log follow-up time as an **offset** (偏移量). It reports the **rate ratio** (率比; treatment vs control). A reduction in AAER is the headline efficacy result.

**Direction and meaning**: **lower AAER = better** — it shows better **disease control** and treatment efficacy. A **rate ratio < 1** means the treatment cuts exacerbations compared with control (e.g. 0.50 ≈ 50% fewer).

Part of the [[Asthma]] therapeutic area. The controller backbone is [[Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)]], and high [[Type 2 Inflammation Biomarkers (Asthma)]] / [[FeNO (Fractional Exhaled Nitric Oxide)]] predict who benefits most.
