# Physical Examination

In a clinical trial the **physical examination (PE, 体格检查)** is a **by-body-system** clinician exam, recorded as **normal / abnormal** per system. Systems in a **complete PE**:

- General appearance (一般情况)
- HEENT — head, eyes, ears, nose, throat (头颈五官)
- Skin (皮肤)
- Lymph nodes (淋巴结)
- Cardiovascular / heart (心血管)
- Respiratory / lungs (呼吸 / 肺)
- Abdomen (腹部)
- Musculoskeletal (肌肉骨骼)
- Neurological (神经系统)
- Extremities (四肢)

Usually paired with — but listed **separately** from — **vital signs** (生命体征: BP, HR, temperature, respiratory rate) and **height/weight**; sometimes [[ECOG Performance Status]].

## Two flavors in the SoA

- **Complete / full PE** — at screening/baseline (and often EOT).
- **Symptom-directed / targeted PE** — focused on relevant systems at routine on-treatment visits.

## Data-handling rule that matters

- An abnormality present at **baseline** → recorded as **medical history**.
- A **new or worsening** abnormality on-treatment → recorded as an **adverse event (AE)**, graded by [[CTCAE Grade]].

So the PE is one of the inputs that **generates AEs**. A row in the [[Schedule of Assessments]]; part of [[Oncology]] safety assessment, alongside [[Eligibility and Safety Parameters (PSMA Radioligand Trial)]].
