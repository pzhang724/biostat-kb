# Investigator's Brochure (IB)

The **Investigator's Brochure** (研究者手册) is the collected body of all clinical + nonclinical (非临床) data on the investigational product that is relevant to studying it in humans. Defined by **ICH E6 (GCP**, Good Clinical Practice, 药物临床试验质量管理规范). It is the investigator's single reference document on the drug.

## What it's for

- To give investigators what they need to **understand and follow the protocol** — rationale (依据), dose, schedule, procedures.
- To support an informed **benefit-risk** (获益-风险) assessment for running the trial and treating subjects.
- To provide the **safety baseline** used during the trial.

## Typical contents

Physicochemical (理化) / pharmaceutical (药学) information · **nonclinical** data (pharmacology 药理学, toxicology 毒理学, animal PK — see [[Pharmacokinetics (PK)]]) · **clinical** data so far (human PK/PD, safety, any efficacy) · a summary + guidance to the investigator.

## Why it matters for safety reporting

The IB carries the **Reference Safety Information (RSI, 参考安全性信息)** — the list of **expected** serious adverse reactions. That list is the yardstick (衡量标准) for "expected vs unexpected" when deciding whether an SAR (serious adverse reaction) is a [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)#SUSAR — the reportable subset|SUSAR]]. So the IB directly controls expedited (加急) reporting.

It is a **living document** — updated regularly (at least once a year) as new data come in. Once the drug is approved, the **label / SmPC** (summary of product characteristics, 产品特性概要) takes over the role of reference safety information. A trial-conduct document used across [[Oncology]].
