Q: 讲一下asthma和COPD分别是什么

A: Asthma — chronic airway inflammatory disease with reversible airflow obstruction (bronchoconstriction that responds to bronchodilators), often linked to allergy/atopy, usually starts in childhood; hallmark is variable, reversible obstruction.

COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease) — progressive disease with largely irreversible airflow limitation, mainly caused by smoking/pollutant exposure, usually diagnosed in older adults; encompasses chronic bronchitis and emphysema, driven more by neutrophilic than eosinophilic inflammation.

Key contrast: reversibility of obstruction (asthma reversible vs. COPD not fully reversible) and typical cause/age of onset (allergy/childhood vs. smoking/older adult).
