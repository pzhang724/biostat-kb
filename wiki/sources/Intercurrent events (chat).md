# Intercurrent events (chat)

Origin: chat Q&A, 2026-06-11 — `raw/sources/2026-06-11 intercurrent events.md`. Follow-up after [[Anti-Cancer Therapy Categories in Oncology Trials]].

- Question: what is an intercurrent event?
- ICH E9(R1) estimand-framework concept: an event after treatment start that affects the interpretation or existence of the outcome.
- Examples: discontinuation/switching, prohibited/rescue meds, subsequent anti-cancer therapy, death (for non-survival endpoints).
- Five pre-specified handling strategies: treatment policy, hypothetical, composite, while on treatment, principal stratum.
- Connection: subsequent anti-cancer therapy is the most common ICE in OS analysis.
