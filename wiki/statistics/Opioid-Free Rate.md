# Opioid-Free Rate

The proportion of subjects who need **no opioid** (阿片类药物) rescue pain relief (rescue analgesia, 补救镇痛) during the assessment window — a key secondary endpoint that shows the **opioid-sparing** (阿片节约) effect of a pain drug in acute pain (急性疼痛) trials.

- Goes together with **total opioid consumption** (often in morphine milligram equivalents, 吗啡毫克当量) and **time to first opioid rescue**.
- Use of a rescue opioid is a classic [[Intercurrent Event]] — it confounds (混杂) the underlying pain reading, so the estimand must say how it is handled.
- Rescue medication is recorded as [[Prior, Concomitant, and Subsequent Treatment]]; in pain trials the opioid rescue is the allowed [[Prohibited and Rescue Medication|rescue medication]].
