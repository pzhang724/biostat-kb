Q: PSA 是什么?

A:
- PSA = Prostate-Specific Antigen (前列腺特异性抗原): a glycoprotein secreted by prostate epithelial cells, measurable in blood.
- Most common biomarker in prostate cancer: used for screening/monitoring, and in trials as an efficacy/progression endpoint.
- Trial endpoints: PSA response (decline >=50%), PSA progression (per PCWG3 — Prostate Cancer Working Group 3 criteria); usually used alongside imaging endpoints (RECIST, bone scan/PET).
- Connection: in prostate cancer trials PSA is an efficacy measure that is easily contaminated by concomitant/subsequent anti-cancer therapy — interpret PSA changes in light of what other anti-cancer therapy the patient received.
