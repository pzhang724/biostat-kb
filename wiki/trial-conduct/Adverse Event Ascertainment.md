# Adverse Event Ascertainment

How AEs are actually **found and recorded** (ascertainment, 确认/获取) during a trial. For AEs with symptoms, the main channel **is** talking between doctor and patient (医患沟通) — but it is not the only one, and everything goes through the investigator's medical judgment.

## Patient communication — two modes

- **Spontaneous** (自发报告) — the patient brings up a complaint by themselves.
- **Solicited** (问询) — at each visit the investigator actively asks an **open-ended, non-leading** question (开放式、非诱导性问题: "any health problems since your last visit?") **instead of reading a symptom checklist** — a checklist would bias / inflate the reporting. (Structured checklists/diaries are kept for specific solicited safety data — e.g. vaccine reactogenicity, 疫苗反应原性 — or for pre-specified [[Adverse Event of Special Interest (AESI)|AESIs]].)

## Non-communication sources

- **What the investigator observes** + the [[Physical Examination|physical exam]].
- **Objective tests** — an abnormal [[Laboratory Safety Panels (Hematology, Chemistry, Coagulation, Urinalysis)|lab]] / vital sign / [[12-Lead Resting ECG|ECG]] that the investigator judges **clinically significant** (有临床意义) becomes an AE (the route triggered by data).
- **Other** — ePRO / patient diaries, phone calls between visits, review of concomitant medications (a new con-med can reveal an AE that was not reported), hospital records.

## The investigator decides

Whatever the source, it is the **investigator** who turns it into an AE record (adjudicates, 判定) — assigning the [[MedDRA]] term, the [[CTCAE Grade|grade]], the [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)|seriousness]], causality, and the [[Action Taken for an Adverse Event|action taken]]. So patient communication is the main *input* for symptoms, but the AE record itself is **judged by the investigator and comes from many sources**.

Ascertainment goes on continuously across the [[Serious Adverse Event (SAE)#Safety reporting period|safety reporting period]]; the recorded *time* is the AE's own onset date, not the visit at which it came up — see [[SDTM, SDTM IG, and Conformance Rules#Events vs Findings timing — how an AE's occurrence is timed|Events vs Findings timing]]. A safety concept used across [[Oncology]].
