Q: 再看 asthma 相关的;说说这些 parameter — FeNO, FEV1 (pre-BD/post-BD), blood eosinophil count,
forced vital capacity, asthma symptom diary, asthma control questionnaire-6, annualized asthma
exacerbation rate, asthma quality of life, european quality of life, interleukin-5, thymus and
activation-regulated chemokine (TARC), IgE, inhaled corticosteroids.

A (grouped — FeNO and pre-BD/post-BD FEV1 already had pages, the rest new):

Lung function (spirometry)
- FEV1 (pre-BD/post-BD): forced expiratory volume in 1 s; pre vs post bronchodilator change = reversibility.
- FVC (forced vital capacity): total forcibly-exhaled volume after max inhalation. FEV1/FVC ratio = the
  obstruction marker (low ratio = obstructive asthma/COPD; low FVC with normal ratio = restrictive).

Type 2 / eosinophilic inflammation biomarkers (T2-high phenotype; each maps to a biologic target)
- FeNO: exhaled nitric oxide, breath biomarker (own page).
- Blood eosinophil count: eosinophils in blood (cells/µL); surrogate for airway eosinophilia; enrichment
  threshold e.g. ≥300; anti-IL-5 lowers it.
- Interleukin-5 (IL-5): Th2/ILC2 cytokine driving eosinophils; target of mepolizumab/reslizumab/benralizumab.
- IgE (immunoglobulin E): antibody of allergic responses; marks allergic asthma; target of omalizumab
  (baseline IgE + weight dose it).
- TARC/CCL17 (thymus and activation-regulated chemokine): Th2-recruiting chemokine; T2 marker, falls with
  anti-IL-4Rα/IL-13 (dupilumab).
  Direction: all elevated = more T2 inflammation; effective T2 therapy lowers them; used to phenotype/enrich/monitor.

Clinical efficacy endpoint
- Annualized asthma exacerbation rate (AAER): exacerbations per patient-year; exacerbation = worsening needing
  systemic/oral steroids (≥3 days) or ER/hospitalization; analyzed by negative binomial regression with log
  follow-up offset → rate ratio. Usual primary endpoint.

Patient-reported outcomes (secondary)
- Asthma symptom diary: daily eDiary of symptoms/rescue use/awakenings; feeds control scores.
- ACQ-6 (Asthma Control Questionnaire-6): 6-item control score 0–6, lower=better, MCID≈0.5 (6-item drops the FEV1 item).
- AQLQ (Asthma Quality of Life Questionnaire): disease-specific QoL 1–7, higher=better, MCID≈0.5.
- EQ-5D (European Quality of Life-5 Dimensions): generic health-utility instrument → utility index + VAS; for
  health economics / QALY, cross-disease comparability.

Background therapy
- Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS): anti-inflammatory controller backbone (budesonide/fluticasone/beclomethasone);
  suppress T2 inflammation → fewer symptoms, better FEV1, fewer exacerbations; high airway dose, low systemic vs
  oral steroids (OCS); often ICS/LABA; high-dose ICS(±OCS) defines severe asthma background for biologics; high
  FeNO/eosinophils predict ICS response.
