# Xerostomia

**Xerostomia (口干)** — dry mouth, the symptom of reduced saliva from impaired **salivary gland** (唾液腺) function.

In the radioligand context it is a **hallmark / common toxicity of PSMA-targeted therapy**: salivary glands **express PSMA**, so [[Lutetium-177 (177Lu)|177Lu]]-/225Ac-PSMA accumulates there and irradiates the glands — an off-target (on-target/off-tumour) effect of where the tracer binds (see [[PSMA PET-CT]], [[Dosimetry]]).

- Tends to be **worse and more dose-limiting** with alpha emitters (225Ac) than with 177Lu.
- Severity graded by [[CTCAE Grade]]; affects quality of life, eating, dental health, and can drive dose reduction / discontinuation.

A toxicity of radioligand therapy in [[Oncology]].
